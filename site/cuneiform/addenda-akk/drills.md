# Drills — the Akkadian signs, three ways

Every sign Cuneiform 103 teaches — new signs and veterans with
their Akkadian readings — as cards: click one, answer out loud
(or on paper — better), then unfold and check. Each sign comes
three ways — *read it*, *write it*, *what does it mean* — and
the deck is interleaved so neighboring cards never come from the
same chapter. This shelf drills only Akkadian: the Sumerian
signs keep [their own deck]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/drills/' | relative_url }}),
and the two never mix.

To drill on paper, open a cut below and print it: the cards
become cut-out slips, ready for a shoebox Leitner system.

## The deck

The deck lies face-down: every tile below holds all the cards,
each in a different order. The marked tile is today's deal.
Sitting down for a real session? Pick any tile — the ones you
have already worked through fade.
