## Where it comes from

The **door** — the lists state it plainly, and the sign's
career agrees: doors and door-leaves travel through it in the
account texts. Akkadian
takes the sound in all three colors, *ig*, *ik*, *iq*.

## How to remember it

A door decides: in or out. In law 2 the door-sign opens the
verdict itself — <span class="norm">iktašassu</span>, "the
River has overwhelmed him" — the moment the case swings shut.
