## Where it comes from

In Sumerian this sign is the night — *ŋi₆*, "night, black"
(stated in the lists). Akkadian never taught it a word of its
own; it took the sound *mi* and put it to work, and the work
turned out to be glorious: *mi* opens
<span class="norm">mīšarum</span>, "justice," the word the
stele names itself with.

## How to remember it

**Night** falls, and out of it comes justice: say *mi* and let
<span class="norm">mīšarum</span> follow. The darkest sign in
the school carries its brightest word.
