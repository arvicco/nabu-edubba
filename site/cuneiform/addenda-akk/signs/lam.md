## Where it comes from

Luxuriance, flourishing growth — the lists' association, graded
unclear; the shape keeps its counsel. In Old Babylonian documents
it earns its rank as the syllable *lam*, most prominently closing
<span class="norm">awīlam</span> — "a man" as the object of the
deed — in the laws' opening formula.

## How to remember it

**Flourish** is what the second man in law 1 was doing until the
accusation landed on him — the object of the sentence, closed by
this sign. In this school's own story, LAM is also the last box
to fall: chapter 06 read law 1 around it, and chapter 07 finally
taught it.
