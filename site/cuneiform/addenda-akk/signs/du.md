## Where it comes from

<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/04-the-verbal-chain/#sign-1207A' | relative_url }}">102's go-sign</a> —
a foot and leg, the walking verb of Sumerian. Crossing the
border it takes a voice you could not guess, ra₂ — and in this
course it appears exactly once per word: closing the
skeleton's compound, 𒄊𒉻𒁺, spoken
<span class="norm">eṣemtum</span>, "bone."

## How to remember it

The **go**-sign ends the bone-word — fitting, since nothing
goes anywhere without bones. A foot-sign at the start, a
foot-sign at the end, and the skeleton in between.
