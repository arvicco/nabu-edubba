## Where it comes from

A corner, a nook — a recess of the world's edge in the lists
(stated; the related word *tubqum* means just that). Note the
school already has a "corner": the Sumerian shelf's IB. Two
different signs, two neighboring senses — this one is the
*nook*, and its Akkadian life is the syllable *ub*, riding second
position in verbs like *u₂-ub-bi-ir-ma*.

## How to remember it

A **nook** is where things tuck in tight: the sign tucks in right
behind the grass-sign in verb after verb, a snug -ub- pocket in
the middle of the action.
