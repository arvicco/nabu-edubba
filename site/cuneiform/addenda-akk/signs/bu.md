## Where it comes from

A long horizontal sign the lists connect with **tearing away**
— graded unclear; the wedges are reticent. Akkadian reads it
*bu* or *pu*, the b and p trading places as freely as they do
on <a href="{{ '/cuneiform/addenda-akk/signs/bi/' | relative_url }}">BI</a>,
and hands it the corpus's most unprovable noun:
<span class="norm">kišpū</span>, "sorcery."

## How to remember it

What cannot be proven must be **torn** out some other way: the
pu of <span class="norm">kišpū</span> is why law 2 sends a man
to the River at all. A long sign for a long walk to the water.
