## Where it comes from

The shekel-sign — about eight grams, a hired worker's month
(stated). There are no coins in Babylon: silver is money by
weight alone, and this sign is the unit the balance-pan
thinks in. Written GIN₂, spoken as the Akkadian word
<span class="norm">šiqlum</span> — from the same weighing-verb
as the laws' <span class="norm">išaqqal</span>.

## How to remember it

The **shekel**: the small weight that prices a month of a
man's work. Sixty of them make the mina; one of them, on this
shelf's loan, is what a mina of silver breeds.
