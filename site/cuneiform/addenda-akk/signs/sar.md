## Where it comes from

The writing-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/16-the-tablet-house-speaks/#sign-122AC' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*sar*, "to write," the verb of the tablet house itself. Akkadian
borrows the sound with a sharpened sibilant: **šar**, and spends
it on the one word the stele says most proudly — *šar-ru-um*,
<span class="norm">šarrum</span>, "king," written out syllable by
syllable where the Sumerian shorthand LUGAL would have done.

## How to remember it

The sign that means **write** spells the king: when Hammurapi
wants his kingship in full Akkadian voice rather than borrowed
Sumerian dress, the scribe's own verb writes the first syllable.
