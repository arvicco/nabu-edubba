## Where it comes from

The milk-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/02-the-case-that-hides/#sign-120B5' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*ga*, milk in the jar. Akkadian keeps the shape and presses the
consonant: **qa**, the emphatic back-of-the-mouth q that Sumerian
never needed a letter for. Where a g and a q could blur, the
scribes let the milk-sign carry the harder sound.

## How to remember it

Milk pressed hard turns to something sharper — so does the g:
press it to the back of the throat and the milk-sign says *qa*.
The stele uses it to call its own words <span class="norm">nasqā</span>,
"choice, picked out" — quality pressed from quantity.
