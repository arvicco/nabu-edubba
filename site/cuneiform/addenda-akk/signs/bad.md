## Where it comes from

A sign of many lives whose picture the lists explain variously —
"to open," "apart," and other readings cluster around it, none
graded certain. Akkadian took the value *be* and put it to
aristocratic work: *be-el* spells <span class="norm">bēl</span>,
"lord (of)," so this modest horizontal wedge-cluster opens the
grandest titles on the stele.

## How to remember it

**Open** — the sign that opens doors opens titles: every
<span class="norm">bēlum</span>, every "lord of…" begins by
opening with *be*. One wedge apart, and the gates of rank swing
wide.
