## Where it comes from

A compound in the lists (U joined with GUD); whatever picture
once lived there has left no confident story. Its Akkadian job
is sharp-edged: **ul**, the negation of plain statements —
where lā bends inside conditions and wishes, ul slams a fact
shut.

## How to remember it

**Never.** Not "not-if," not "may-it-not" — just no. The king
uses it exactly once in the lines this course reads, to say his
deeds have no rival, and the sentence sounds like a door
closing.
