## Where it comes from

A compound in the catalogs — a lion-sign with "day" pressed into
it (PIRIG×UD) — read as a raging beast, a lion in fury; the lists
state the picture without certifying it. Akkadian uses the sound:
*ug* or *uk*, voiced or not as the word demands, most memorably
in law 1's *uk-ti-in*, "he convicted."

## How to remember it

A **lion** is what a courtroom becomes at the moment of
conviction: *uktīn* — "he proved it against him" — opens with the
lion-sign, and someone is about to be eaten.
