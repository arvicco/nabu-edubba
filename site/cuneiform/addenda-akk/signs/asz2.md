## Where it comes from

The sign lists associate this sign with the Sumerian word for a
**curse** — an identification we grade cautiously, since the
shape itself argues nothing. Akkadian uses it as the bare
syllable *as*, indifferent to the menace.

## How to remember it

A curse, mid-sentence and defused: on the stele the bitter sign
sits inside <span class="norm">nasqā</span>, "are choice" — the
king praising his own words. Say *as* and note the irony; irony
is memorable.
