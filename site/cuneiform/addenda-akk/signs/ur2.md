## Where it comes from

The lap and the loins — a body's base — and from there the
base of anything: a mountain's foot, a temple's foundations
(stated). Akkadian borrows the sound for plain syllable duty.

## How to remember it

The **lap** is where things rest and where they start. In this
course it carries one unforgettable rest: the last syllable of
<span class="norm">līmur</span>, "let him see his case" — the
course's whole purpose, coming to rest on its last sign.
