## Where it comes from

The lists connect this sign with a **corner** — graded unclear;
the wedges keep their own counsel. What is certain is its range:
Akkadian lets it write *ib*, *eb* and *ep* alike, the vowel
bending to the word around it — the same easy vowel-blur the
school met in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/addenda-akk/signs/ir/' | relative_url }}">IR</a>.

## How to remember it

A corner joins two walls; this sign joins vowels. It opens the
king's <span class="norm">epšētum</span>, "deeds," and it will
close the stretch's last blessing, <span class="norm">liṭīb</span>
— "may it be well." Deeds first, blessing after: a fair order.
