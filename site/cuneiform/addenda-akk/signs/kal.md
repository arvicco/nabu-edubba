## Where it comes from

102's mighty-sign — Sumerian *kal(ag)*, "strong, precious."
Akkadian reads it *dan*, and for once the borrowed sound and the
old meaning shake hands: <span class="norm">dannum</span>, "the
strong (one)," opens on it in the most quoted line of Babylonian
law — the commission that the strong not wrong the weak.

## How to remember it

**Mighty** in Sumerian, *dan-* of <span class="norm">dannum</span>
in Akkadian — the one veteran whose new voice says exactly what
the sign always meant. Strength crossing the border unbroken.
