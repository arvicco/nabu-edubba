## Where it comes from

The king-sign of <a href="{{ '/cuneiform/101/12-reference/#sign-12217' | relative_url }}">101</a> — *lugal*, the "big man" of the
earliest city-states. Akkadian kings kept the sign and dropped
the sound: on the stele it is written LUGAL, a **sumerogram**,
and read out as the Akkadian word
<span class="norm">šarrum</span>, "king" — or, standing before
a name it governs, as the construct <span class="norm">šar</span>.

## How to remember it

The most royal sign in the school never changes its meaning,
only its language. Where Sumerian said *lugal*, Babylon says
<span class="norm">šarrum</span> — same wedges, new king.
