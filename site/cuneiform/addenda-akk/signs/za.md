## Where it comes from

The bead-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/13-you-are/#sign-1235D' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*za*, the little stone of worth. Akkadian presses the sibilant:
**ṣa**, the emphatic ṣ of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/103/02-the-noun-wears-its-case/' | relative_url }}">the bile-sign's</a>
family — and sets it working in the money-verb of every loan:
<span class="norm">uṣṣab</span>, "it will add, it will grow."

## How to remember it

A **bead** is wealth in its smallest unit — and *uṣṣab* is what
wealth does in a loan contract: bead by bead, shekel by shekel,
the interest grows. The pressed s is the sound of the count
tightening.
