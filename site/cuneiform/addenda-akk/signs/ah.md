## Where it comes from

A workhorse whose picture keeps its secret (unclear) — the
lists hand it straight to the ear: one sign for the throat's
breath in all three colors, aḫ, eḫ, uḫ. Akkadian, rich in the
ḫ-sound Sumerian barely used, keeps it busy.

## How to remember it

A **sigh** — the same breath whether it rides an a, an e, or a
u. One sign, three vowels, one rasp in the throat.
