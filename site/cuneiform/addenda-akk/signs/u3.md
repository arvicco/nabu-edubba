## Where it comes from

A compound in the old lists (IGI.DIB in the modern sign catalogs)
whose pieces long ago fused into one workaday shape. Its career is
its sound: *u₃* writes the Akkadian conjunction *u*, "and."
Sumerian mostly joined things by setting them side by side;
Akkadian, like its Semitic cousins, says the "and" out loud — so
a sign Sumerian could take or leave became one of the fourteen
most frequent in Old Babylonian writing.

## How to remember it

**And** — the sign you'll meet exactly where English would put
the word: between heaven *and* earth, the strong *and* the weak,
silver *and* barley. When the line pauses to add one more thing,
this is the wedge-cluster doing it.
