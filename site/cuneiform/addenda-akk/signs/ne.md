## Where it comes from

The brazier-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/04-the-verbal-chain/#sign-12248' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*ne*, fire in its bowl. Among the readings Akkadian inherited is
**bi**, and Old Babylonian gives it the most-written beat in the
whole mailbag: the middle syllable of
<span class="norm">qibīma</span>, "speak!" — the second word of
nearly every letter ever posted in Babylon.

## How to remember it

A letter is read ALOUD — the reader lends the sender a voice the
way a brazier lends a room its light. The fire-sign burning in
the middle of "speak!" is the letter coming alight.
