## Where it comes from

The verdict-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/14-a-row-of-proverbs/#sign-12072' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*di*, the court's word. Akkadian adds the pressed dental:
**ṭi**, the emphatic ṭ that
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/103/01-sound-by-sound/' | relative_url }}">103</a>
taught your mouth to hold — and spends it on a wish:
<span class="norm">liṭīb</span>, "may it be sweet, may he make
well."

## How to remember it

A **verdict** can be pressed harder: say di with the tongue
braced and it comes out ṭi — the sound of a judgment you can
feel. The stele wishes its reader's heart *liṭīb*, sweetened —
a verdict in the reader's favor.
