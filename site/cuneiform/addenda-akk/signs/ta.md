## Where it comes from

<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/00-orientation/#sign-122EB' | relative_url }}">102's from-sign</a> —
Sumerian's ablative marker, the little "from, out of" of the
case chain. Akkadian takes only the sound — and hands it the
biggest job of the sign's life: [ta] is the marker of the
perfect tense, pressed in after a root's first consonant to
say "it HAS happened."

## How to remember it

**From** in Sumerian; in Akkadian, the -ta- of the record —
the syllable that turns "he struck" into "he has struck."
What happened comes FROM somewhere: the deed stands behind
the speaker.
