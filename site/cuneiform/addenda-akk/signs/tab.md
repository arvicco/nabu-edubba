## Where it comes from

For once the sign is exactly what it looks like: **two wedges
lying side by side** — a pair, and by extension "to double"
(stated in the lists, and visibly true). Akkadian reads it
*tab* or *tap*, the final consonant softening to fit the word,
as Akkadian spelling likes to do.

## How to remember it

Two strokes, one sign: a **pair**. Where you see it, something
is doubled or carried along — in
<span class="norm">itabbal</span>, "he carries off," the pair
rides in the middle of the verb.
