## Where it comes from

The foot and the path it walks (classic) — Sumerian *giri₃*
meant both. In this course the sign never speaks alone: it
opens the skeleton's own compound, 𒄊𒉻𒁺 — written
GIR₃.PAD.RA₂, spoken as ONE Akkadian word,
<span class="norm">eṣemtum</span>, "bone."

## How to remember it

A **path** begins with a foot, and a foot stands on bone. Three
signs walk in file — foot, morsel, go — and the reader says
none of them: only the word they carry together.
