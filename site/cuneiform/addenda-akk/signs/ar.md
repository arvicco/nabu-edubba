## Where it comes from

The lists build it from the eye-sign and read it as praise,
glory (stated) — being SEEN well, which is what glory is. In
Akkadian text it works as the plain syllable ar, busiest
inside <span class="norm">maḫar</span>, "before, facing."

## How to remember it

**Praise** is standing before approving eyes — and the sign's
big word, maḫar, is exactly that: standing before. An eye in
the sign, an audience in the meaning.
