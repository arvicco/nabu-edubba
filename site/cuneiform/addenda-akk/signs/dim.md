## Where it comes from

A post or pillar in the sign lists — stated, as so often. Its
Akkadian value *tim* made it a fixture of the written landscape:
feminine and other -t- nouns in the genitive end in -tim
(*mātim*, "of the land"; *erṣetim*, "of the earth"), so this sign
holds up the back end of prepositional phrases all over the
corpus.

## How to remember it

A **post** is what things are fastened *to* — and the genitive is
the case of belonging. Where the post stands at a word's end,
the word is tied to something: of the land, of the earth, of the
year.
