## Where it comes from

A plant — grass, greenery — in the sign lists; Sumerian
read it *u₂* for plants and food. Akkadian took the bare vowel
and made it a verbal workhorse: a whole family of verb forms
opens on *u₂-* (*u₂-ub-bi-ir-ma*, "he accused"; *u₂-ki-in*, "he
established"), so the little grass-sign stands at the head of
action after action in the laws.

## How to remember it

**Grass** grows first — before anything else happens on a plot,
the grass is already up. In the laws it is the same: before the
verb gets anywhere, the grass-sign has already sprouted at its
front.
