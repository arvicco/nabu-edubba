## Where it comes from

The lists associate it with scent and perfume — stated, not
certain. In Akkadian hands it does double duty: the same sign
writes both *ir* and *er*, because Old Babylonian spelling
tolerated a blur between i and e that the language itself often
made. Context settles which you are hearing; the sign doesn't
care.

## How to remember it

A **scent** drifts — you can't pin it to one spot, and this sign
won't be pinned to one vowel. Catch the general direction (r,
front vowel) and follow the word to its meaning.
