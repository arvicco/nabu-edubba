## Where it comes from

Prince, noble — one of the script's honest old rank-signs, the
identification classic since the earliest lists. Akkadian keeps
both the dignity and the sound: as a syllable it is *nun*, and
its most famous posting is inside the name of the great gods
collectively — the A-*nun*-na-ki, "the princely offspring."

## How to remember it

A **prince** among syllables: it turns up where rank is being
handed out. The gods who assign kingship on the stele's second
line carry the prince-sign in their own family name.
