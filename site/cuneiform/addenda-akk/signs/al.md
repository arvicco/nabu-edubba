## Where it comes from

The **hoe** — the classic identification, and for once the
tradition is loud: Sumerian literature sings an actual hymn to
the hoe, the tool that breaks the ground and builds the city.
Akkadian borrows the sign as a plain syllable, and the old
farmhand ends up inside the law's most patient verbs.

## How to remember it

A hoe strikes the ground step after step, the way a durative
verb keeps happening: <span class="norm">illak</span>, "he
goes," <span class="norm">itabbal</span>, "he carries off" —
both close on the hoe. Slow, repeated, thorough work.
