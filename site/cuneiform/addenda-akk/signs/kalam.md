## Where it comes from

The Land-sign of <a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/07-i-am-king/#sign-12327' | relative_url }}">102</a> — *kalam*, Sumer's own name for
itself. Akkadian did not keep its sound at all: it kept the
sign whole, as a **sumerogram** — a word written in Sumerian
and read out in Akkadian. On the clay it is KALAM; in the
scribe's mouth it is <span class="norm">mātum</span>, "the
land," in whatever case the sentence demands.

## How to remember it

Nothing to relearn: the sign still *means* the Land. Only the
voice changes at the border — the eye reads Sumerian, the mouth
answers in Akkadian. When you meet it in a reading, say
<span class="norm">mātum</span> and move on.
