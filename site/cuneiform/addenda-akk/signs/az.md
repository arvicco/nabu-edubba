## Where it comes from

The lists build it as a lion-sign with a stone locked inside,
and read the whole as the **bear** (stated) — Sumer wrote the
animal, and Akkadian kept the growl: as, az, and the pressed
aṣ, all one sign.

## How to remember it

A caged beast: the lion-frame with a stone in its belly. Its
growl is the snap at the end of
<span class="norm">imtaḫaṣ</span>, "he has struck" — the bear
closes the blow.
