## Where it comes from

<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/101/12-reference/#sign-121A0' | relative_url }}">101's place-sign</a> —
earth and ground, the sign under every city name. In Akkadian
text it works twice over: as a plain syllable
(<em>ki-nu-ni</em>, "of the brazier-festival"), and — in the
contracts' Sumerian formulary — as its old self, opening
"from (the hands of) so-and-so."

## How to remember it

**Place** twice over: the ground a festival stands on when it
is a syllable, and the place a loan comes FROM when the
notary's old Sumerian does the talking.
