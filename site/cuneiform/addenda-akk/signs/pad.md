## Where it comes from

A morsel, a bit, a daily ration (stated) — on administrative
tablets this sign hands out bread. In this course it never
speaks alone: it is the middle of the skeleton's compound,
𒄊𒉻𒁺 — a piece, a bit, a bone — spoken only inside the one
word <span class="norm">eṣemtum</span>.

## How to remember it

The **morsel** in the middle: between the foot that walks and
the go-sign that closes, a small hard piece — the bit of a
body, the bone.
