## Where it comes from

Dust, soil, rubble — so the sign lists, with the usual measured
confidence. Its Akkadian career runs on the sound: *iš* is a
vowel-consonant syllable, exactly the shape Akkadian's closed
syllables demand, and it opens verb after verb in the past tense
(*iš-tu*, *iš-ru-uk* — "from," "he granted") along with nouns
like *išdum*, "foundation."

## How to remember it

**Dust** is what a foundation trench is dug through and what
everything settles into. The sign starts at the bottom: where
there is an *iš*, something is being grounded — a building's
footing or a verb's past.
