## Where it comes from

The child-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/101/12-reference/#sign-12309' | relative_url }}">101</a>
— *tur*, "small, young," and *dumu*, "child, son." Akkadian kept
the second reading whole as a **sumerogram**: on the stele it is
DUMU, in the mouth it is <span class="norm">mārum</span>, "son"
— construct <span class="norm">mār</span>, endlessly stacked in
names and laws alike.

## How to remember it

The smallest sign in the family carries the family: where a
son's rights, body or inheritance are at stake, the little
child-sign stands in the line — and Babylon says
<span class="norm">mārum</span>.
