## Where it comes from

The knowing-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/03-say-it-twice/#sign-1236A' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*zu*, "to know." Akkadian adds a softened sibling, **su**, and
gives it a very particular beat: the suffix "him/his" after a
dental or sibilant, where <span class="norm">-šu</span> melts —
<span class="norm">iktašad + šu</span> becomes
<span class="norm">iktašassu</span>, and the knowing-sign writes
what the melt leaves behind.

## How to remember it

To **know** someone is to have caught them: when the law's verb
closes its grip on "him" — <span class="norm">iktašassu</span>,
"it has overwhelmed HIM" — the knowing-sign seals the capture.
