## Where it comes from

Bile, gall — the bitter fluid — per the sign lists.
Akkadian drafted it for a sound of its own: the emphatic *ṣ*, a
pressed, sharp s that belongs to the Semitic side of the family
and had no Sumerian sign waiting for it. The scribes' solution
across the syllabary was to repurpose signs near the right sound;
this one took *ṣi* and *ṣe* and appears in the stele's very first
line.

## How to remember it

**Bile** is sharp and it stings — so is the sound. When you see
the bile-sign, press the s until it bites: ṣ, the consonant with
an edge.
