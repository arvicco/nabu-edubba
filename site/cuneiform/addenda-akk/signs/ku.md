## Where it comes from

The seizing-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/14-a-row-of-proverbs/#sign-121AA' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*ku*, to grasp and to lay down. Akkadian presses the k back to
**qu**, and the epilogue reaches for it in a word about worth:
<span class="norm">šūqurātim</span>, "precious" — literally
"made dear," the causative of being rare.

## How to remember it

What is **seized** is held dear: press the k until it grips —
qu — and you are inside <span class="norm">šūqurātim</span>,
the king's own word for words worth keeping.
