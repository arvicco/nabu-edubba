## Where it comes from

<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/15-i-am-the-king/#sign-12305' | relative_url }}">102's birth-sign</a> — Sumerian *tu(d)*, "to be born." Akkadian
enlisted it for a sound Sumerian never separated out: the
emphatic *ṭ*, the pressed t of the Semitic consonant row. As
*ṭu₂* it stands at the head of
<span class="norm">ṭubbim</span>, "to make well" — the last word
of the stele's statement of purpose.

## How to remember it

**Born** again with a harder edge: the gentle t of birth pressed
into ṭ. What is well-being — <span class="norm">ṭūbum</span> —
if not being born into the world and kept whole in it? The
birth-sign carries the king's promise to keep it so.
