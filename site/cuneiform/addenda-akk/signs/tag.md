## Where it comes from

To touch, to strike — the sign's stated core in the lists, with a
spread of crafts (weaving, dyeing) hanging off it. Akkadian
borrowed its reading *šum* and spent it on one incomparable job:
<em>šum-ma</em>, <span class="norm">šumma</span>, "if" — the
first word of almost three hundred laws.

Keep it apart from its near-twin on the Sumerian shelf: the
give-sign <a href="{{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/sum/' | relative_url }}">SUM</a>
reads *šum₂* — same sound to the ear, different sign on the clay,
and the index digit is the field's fence between them.

## How to remember it

**Touch** — every law begins by touching a hypothetical: *if* a
man… The lightest touch of the stylus opens a whole world of
consequence; that touch is this sign.
