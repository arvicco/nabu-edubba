## Where it comes from

A **branch** — the classic identification, and Sumerian already
saw the politics in it: the same sign read as the scepter, the
branch that rules. Akkadian keeps the plain sound *pa* and lets
it serve wherever needed — including inside the verdict-verbs
of the talion laws.

## How to remember it

A branch in the court's hand: <span class="norm">uḫappadū</span>,
"they shall blind," carries the pa at its center — the scepter
come down. Power was always a stick.
