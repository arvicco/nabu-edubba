## Where it comes from

The lists offer no picture worth repeating — this is a working
syllable, not a former thing. Its keep is earned in verbs: *il*
opens <span class="norm">illak</span>, "he goes," the plainest
and most travelled verb of Babylonian law.

## How to remember it

One **step**: *il*. Then another: <span class="norm">illak</span>
— "he goes," off to the river, to the judge, to wherever the
law sends him. The syllable that sets the whole stele walking.
