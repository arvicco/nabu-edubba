## Where it comes from

The lists connect this sign with **approaching** — graded
unclear, like most of the oldest identifications. Its course
career is a small drama: it stood one box wide in the ordeal's
acquittal for four chapters before the course handed it over.

## How to remember it

The sign that kept you waiting: it **approached** for four
chapters and arrived last. It completes
<span class="norm">ūtebbibaššuma</span> — "the River has
cleared him" — the best news in law 2.
