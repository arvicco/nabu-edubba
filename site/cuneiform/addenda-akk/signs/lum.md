## Where it comes from

The lists connect it with ripening and abundance — to be
fruitful, laden — though the picture behind the wedges is graded
unclear. Its Akkadian job is steady beyond argument: *lum*, the
syllable that closes <span class="norm">awīlum</span> in the
nominative — which means it closes the subject of nearly every
law on the stele.

## How to remember it

**Ripen** — a man in full legal standing is, in the laws' eyes, a
case fully ripe: rights, duties, liabilities all grown in. The
word for him ripens to its end on this sign.
