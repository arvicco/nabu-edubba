## Where it comes from

A round impression — in origin a big circle of the round stylus
end, the number **3600**: sixty sixties, the ceiling of the
sexagesimal world (stated). Where we say "a million thanks,"
the scribes wrote one round sign and meant *beyond counting*.

## How to remember it

The **myriad**: one circle holding sixty sixties. When a friend
wishes you MU ŠAR₂.KAM — year number 3600 — he is wishing you
more calendar than arithmetic can pay out.
