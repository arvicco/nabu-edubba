## Where it comes from

A reed bundle, looped at the top — the gate-post standard of
the goddess, planted at her shrines since before writing could
spell a name (classic). It IS her signature: Sumerian Inanna,
and in Akkadian mouths <span class="norm">Ištar</span> — one
sign, two names, one Lady of the morning star.

## How to remember it

The **goddess** at the gate: her looped standard stood at the
storehouse door, and the sign still stands wherever she is
named — in hymns, in temples, and in the year-name of a
Sippar loan.
