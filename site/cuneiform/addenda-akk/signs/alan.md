## Where it comes from

A standing figure — the statue, a person's image made to
outlast them (classic). Sumerian called it alan; Akkadian
keeps the writing and speaks its own word,
<span class="norm">ṣalmum</span> — image, likeness, the stone
self that receives what is said before it.

## How to remember it

The **statue** is the person who stays. Hammurapi's laws end
with an invitation to stand before HIS — ana maḫar ṣalmīya —
and read. The sign is the king waiting in stone.
