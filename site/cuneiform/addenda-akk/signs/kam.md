## Where it comes from

A compound in the lists (HI pressed with BAD); its life is
purely grammatical — the **counting tag**: hung after a
numeral, it turns "year 3600" into "the 3600th year," the way
English hangs a -th. Date formulas live on it: year-names,
month-counts, the bookkeeping of time itself.

## How to remember it

The **ordinal** hook: number + KAM = "-th." Wherever the
scribes counted anything in order — and they counted everything
— this little tag closes the phrase.
