## Where it comes from

The distant-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/10-in-those-days/#sign-12291' | relative_url }}">102</a> —
*ri*, what is far off. Akkadian, which cares about the difference
between i and e, lets the same sign say both: **re** is the
e-colored twin, met first in a proud name —
<span class="norm">Sîn-ašarēd</span>, "Sîn is foremost."

## How to remember it

What is **distant** is out ahead: the one who has gone farthest
in front is <span class="norm">ašarēd</span>, "foremost" — and
the far-sign, e-colored, sits inside the word for it.
