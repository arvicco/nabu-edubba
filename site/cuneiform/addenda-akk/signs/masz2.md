## Where it comes from

The kid goat (stated) — and, by the oldest arithmetic there
is, the interest on a loan: a herd grows by births, so the
scribes named a loan's growth after the newborn animal. Silver
bears shekels the way goats bear kids.

## How to remember it

The **kid** in the ledger: every mina out on loan is a nanny
goat, and MAŠ₂ counts what she bears. Where this sign appears,
money is breeding.
