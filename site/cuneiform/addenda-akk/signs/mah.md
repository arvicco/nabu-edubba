## Where it comes from

<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/07-i-am-king/#sign-12224' | relative_url }}">102's majesty-word</a> —
Sumerian *maḫ*, "great, exalted," the word behind lofty temple
names. Akkadian keeps the exact sound and spends it on plain
syllable work — including, in law 202, the middle of a very
unexalted word: sixty lashes.

## How to remember it

**Exalted** in Sumerian, workhorse [maḫ] in Akkadian — a
grandee doing day labor. The grandeur is still there when the
scribes need it; on most tablets it just carries its sound.
