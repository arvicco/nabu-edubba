## Where it comes from

A day-sign with a crab-like companion pressed against it; the
lists read the pair as **spittle, phlegm** (stated) — scribes
did not flinch from the body. Akkadian borrows the plain sound
*uh* and gives the unlovely sign the most famous verb it owns.

## How to remember it

Nobody forgets an insult: the spit-sign opens
<span class="norm">uḫtappid</span>, "he has blinded" — the deed
of law 196, an eye for an eye. Crude sign, capital crime,
unforgettable pairing.
