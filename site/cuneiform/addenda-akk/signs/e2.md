## Where it comes from

The house-sign of
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/101/12-reference/#sign-1208D' | relative_url }}">101</a>
— *e₂*, the house and household, the sign in this school's own
name. Akkadian kept it whole as a **sumerogram**: on the clay it
is E₂, in the mouth it is <span class="norm">bītum</span>,
"house, household, estate," declined and suffixed like any
Akkadian noun — <span class="norm">bīssu</span>, "his estate,"
with the -šu melted on.

## How to remember it

The oldest address in the school: you learned this sign to read
*é-dub-ba-a*, the tablet house. Babylon still lives in it — it
only answers to a different word at the door.
