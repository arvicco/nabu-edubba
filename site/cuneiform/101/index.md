# Cuneiform 101 · Foundations

**The first course of the cuneiform school, and of Edubba itself.**
No prerequisites. By the end you will know how cuneiform works as a
writing system, write and recognize a first inventory of signs, and —
the promise that matters — **read real royal inscriptions** written
four thousand years ago, in the original.

This course is about cuneiform itself, not yet about one language:
the literacy tracks for [Sumerian and Akkadian](../) build on it.
