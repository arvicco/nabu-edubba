# Easily confused — side by side

Some signs blur precisely because you know both: each alone is
easy, and the pair is the problem. The cure is to look at them
together, where telling them apart is the whole exercise. Take a
row below, say what separates its signs — a wedge more, a head
turned, a stroke that crosses — then check yourself: either sign
opens its full story on the Signs shelf. When [the deck]({{
'/cuneiform/addenda/drills/' | relative_url }}) deals you one of
these and you hesitate, this page is where to come back to.

The lookalikes, deliberately together: tell them apart before
they blur.
