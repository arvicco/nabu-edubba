## Where it comes from

A spelling workhorse with no picture-story to tell — the sign
lists offer nothing worth teaching. It earns its keep as the
syllable *ma* in names, loanwords, and grammar (the *-ma-* "for
me" ring of verb chains), on thousands of tablets.

## How to remember it

Every stable needs its **workhorse**: unglamorous, strong, in
harness every single day. This sign has no myth, no picture,
no famous word of its own — and the corpus would stop moving
without it.

- **ma-na** — the mina, spelled sound by sound: two workhorse
  syllables carrying a unit of silver

ancient-history book — plain spelling doing durable work. The
