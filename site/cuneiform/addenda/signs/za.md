## Where it comes from

*Za* is bead, precious stone — the lapis-and-carnelian ledger
word. Three wedges only. Its star turn is grammatical: *za-e*,
"you" — the pronoun that lets a hymn look its addressee in the
eye.

## How to remember it

A **bead** is small, hard, and precious — three wedges you could
string on a cord. And the person a hymn calls *you* is precious
the same way: *za-e*, the bead-word aimed at someone who
matters.
