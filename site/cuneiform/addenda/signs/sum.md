## Where it comes from

A bundle of onions in origin, the standard lists say — garden
produce, handed over. From that handing: *šum₂*, "to give," the
giving verb of gods and administrators alike. Rations are given,
kingship is given, children are given — one verb serves all the
generosity in the corpus.

## How to remember it

To **give** is to hold out the bundle: the sign still looks like
stalks tied at the middle, offered across a counter. From onions
to empires, the gesture never changed.

Piece by piece:

- **an-na** — An, the sky-god, as the giver
- **šum₂** — gave

A whole gratitude compressed into a personal name: this child
was given. The name is read in
