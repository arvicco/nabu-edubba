## Where it comes from

Court records say *di* for a case: lawsuit, judgment — *di kud*,
"to cut a verdict," is how Sumerian decides. The proverbs use
its going-sense instead, for anyone (or any dog) that wanders.
Justice and strolling, one syllable apart.

## How to remember it

A **verdict** is a line drawn through a dispute — one straight
judgment. And till the judgment comes, the case *goes on*: the
same sign ambles through the proverbs as plain going.

- **ur** — the dog
- **i-im-** — the verb chain's running start
- **di** — goes about: this sign, off its bench and strolling
