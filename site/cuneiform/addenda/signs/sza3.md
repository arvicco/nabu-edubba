## Where it comes from

*Ša₃* is the heart — and the midst: where feelings, thoughts,
and the middle of anything live. Put water's sign before it and
you get *a-ša₃*, "field" — the plot at the heart of the water.
Literary spelling often shows the fuller shape *šag₄*.

## How to remember it

The **heart** sits in the middle of everything, and so does this
sign's meaning: the inside of a person, a city, a month. When a
text goes inward — moods, wombs, interiors — the heart-sign is
already there.

Piece by piece:

- **šag₄** — the heart
- **{d}en-lil₂-la₂** — of Enlil
- **gu₂-bi nam-gi₄** — overflowed its banks, a river's image for
  a god's feeling

A god's heart in flood — the interior made a landscape. The line
