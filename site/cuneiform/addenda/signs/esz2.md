## Where it comes from

The sign lists give the picture as a rope (stated, not certain).
Its grammar job is what the courses lean on: *-še₃*, the
terminative tag — "to, toward, for the purpose of." It closes
countless dedications: *nam-ti-la-ni-še₃*, "for his life."

## How to remember it

A **rope** ties one thing TO another — which is exactly what the
tag does: it ropes a phrase to its goal. Four wedges, a short
lasso thrown toward a purpose.

Piece by piece:

- **nam-ti** — life, the abstraction of living
- **-la-ni** — his, with a spelling-glue syllable
- **-še₃** — for: the rope tying the whole gift to its purpose

Why build a temple? *For his life* — and the rope-sign makes the
purpose stick. The formula is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/11-a-dedication-for-real/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 11</a>.
