## Where it comes from

A foot and leg, by the classic reading of the picture — a
person's own legs, doing the going. It writes *du*, "to go"; in
administrative lines the same sign regularly means "delivered,
brought," and it carries the further reading *tum₂*, "to bring."
Motion in every direction, one leg.

## How to remember it

**Go**: a leg mid-stride, heel up. Whether a person goes or a
jar of oil is walked to the palace, the script sends the same
foot to do it.

Piece by piece:

- **i₃** — oil
- **e₂-gal** — the palace, the "big house"
- **DU** — delivered: the foot-sign as the receipt's whole verb
