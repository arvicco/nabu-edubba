## Where it comes from

Commonly connected with an oil jar — a fat-bellied vessel, the
account books' favorite commodity after grain. As *i₃* it IS oil,
butter, fat; as *ni* it is a plain syllable; and at the head of a
verb chain *i₃-* is a neutral little starter that just says *the
verb begins here*.

## How to remember it

An **oil** jar tips forward, ready to pour. Oil slicks into
everything — food, lamps, offerings, and half the verb chains you
will read, where the jar quietly primes the verb.

Piece by piece:

- **niŋ₂ ak-zu** — the things you do: thing, do, your
- **a-ba** — who?
- **i₃-zu-zu** — can know them: this sign starting the chain,
  *zu* doubled for "ever really know"

Awe before a god, and the oil jar starts the final verb. The line
is read in
