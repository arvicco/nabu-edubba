## Where it comes from

Another honest picture: an eye, turned on its side by the
script's rotation. *Igi* is eye and face — and by extension
"before, in front of": a witness is named *igi PN*, "before
So-and-so's eye." Court records run on this sign.

## How to remember it

An **eye** with its lid and pupil, lying on its side the way
all the old pictures ended up. What the eye has seen, the
document can prove — "before the eye of" is how the archives
say *witnessed*.

Piece by piece:

- **piriŋ igi huš** — a lion with a fierce eye: the lion and the
  fierceness await their signs
- **ušumgal-e tud-da-me-en** — born of a dragon am I
