## Where it comes from

*Kal* is "mighty, strong" — the royal epithet *nitah kalag-ga*,
"mighty male," follows kings' names through the entire Ur III
titulary — and also "precious, rare": what is strong holds its
value. On seals, the name *lu₂-kal-la*, "the precious one,"
wears the same sign.

## How to remember it

**Mighty** and precious are one wedge-cluster: strength you can
bank. A king is *kalag-ga* the way a treasure is *kal* — held
tight, worth the holding.

Piece by piece:

- **niŋ₂ nam-kal-kal-en** — if you treasure a thing
- **niŋ₂-e me-kal-kal** — the thing treasures you back: *kal*
  doubled on both sides of the deal

The oldest advice about caring for your tools, hinged on this
sign twice over. The saying is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/16-the-tablet-house-speaks/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 16</a>.
