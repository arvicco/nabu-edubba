## Where it comes from

Pictorial origin not transparent — a grammar workhorse from its
first appearance, with no picture to lean on. Hung on a person's
name or title, *-ra* is the dative: "to, for (someone)." Gifts,
prayers, and dedications all travel on it.

## How to remember it

**Toward** someone: the sign leans its wedges forward like a
messenger already moving. When you meet *-ra*, someone is about
to receive something — find out who.

Piece by piece:

- **ama** — mother
- **diŋir-re-ne** — of the gods
- **-ra** — for her: the dative sealing the address

A whole temple, handed over on one syllable of direction. The
dedication is read in
