## Where it comes from

The sign behind Sumerian epic's favorite way to begin: *ud
ri-a* / *ud re-a*, "in those distant days." As a demonstrative
it points far away; as a verb it is "to pour out, to cast upon"
— what the Great Mountain does with seed in Gudea's hymn.

## How to remember it

**Distant** is where the storyteller points: over the horizon,
before the flood, in THAT day — not this one. When a poem opens
with this sign, settle in: you are being carried a long way
back.
