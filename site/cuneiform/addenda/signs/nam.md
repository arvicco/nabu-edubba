## Where it comes from

Its pictorial origin is stated variously across the sign lists —
the job matters more than the etymology. *Nam* is fate, destiny;
and prefixed to a concrete word it builds the abstraction:
*nam-lugal*, kingship; *nam-ti*, life; *nam-mah*, majesty. The
gods decide fates by *cutting* them — *nam tar*.

## How to remember it

**Fate** is the -ness of things: put *nam-* in front and a king
becomes kingship, a life becomes Life. Fate is what you get when
the concrete is lifted one shelf up — and the gods keep the
knife.

Piece by piece:

- **ud** — on the day
- **an ki-a** — in heaven and earth
- **nam tar-re-da** — when destinies were being cut

The first line of the greatest Sumerian building hymn opens with
fate on the cutting table. It opens
