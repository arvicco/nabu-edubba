## Where it comes from

The majesty word: *mah* is "great, exalted" — not the everyday
big of [GAL]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/gal/' | relative_url }})
but the ceremonial rank above it. Combine it with the
abstraction-maker and you get *nam-mah*, "majesty, greatness" —
a word the hymns cannot do without.

## How to remember it

**Exalted** stands on a platform: where *gal* just measures,
*mah* bows. Keep the pair straight by register — a big jar is
*gal*; a great god is *mah*.
