## Where it comes from

Classic origin: the sign lists connect it with the milk jug and
dairy work. As a word it is *ga*, "milk"; as a syllable it
spells its sound wherever needed — including at the front of
some of the oldest personal names we can read.

## How to remember it

A **milk** jug in its crate: the sign's wedges rack up like
jars in a dairy. Milk is the first food and *ga* is early
everywhere — in the earliest names, it is what children are
called after.

Piece by piece:

- **ga** — milk
- **naŋ-naŋ** — drink, drink

A nursery name from Šuruppak, forty-six centuries old, and it
still sounds like a lullaby. The name is read in
