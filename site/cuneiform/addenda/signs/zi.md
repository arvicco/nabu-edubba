## Where it comes from

*Zi* is breath and life — and, as an adjective, "true,
righteous": the word behind every "true shepherd" and "true
house" in the hymns. Literary texts often spell the fuller shape
*zid*. What breathes true, is true — the meanings share one
chest.

## How to remember it

**Breath** is the proof of life and the test of truth: hold a
blade of grass to the lips and see it move. Where a king is
called true, this sign is doing the certifying.
