## Where it comes from

A fish that kept its fins through the wedge era — the classic
picture is still legible. As a word it is the fish of the
ponds and offering lists; by pure sound it fronts a verb as
*ha-*, the wish-prefix: "may it be so."

## How to remember it

A **fish** grants wishes in half the world's stories — and in
Sumerian it literally does: put the fish before a verb and the
verb becomes a hope. Fins for swimming, sound for wishing.

Piece by piece:

- **šag₄-zu** — your heart
- **ki-bi** — to its place
- **ha-ma-gi₄-gi₄** — may it return for me: the fish-wish, the
  "for me" ring, the homecoming verb doubled

The standard consolation, opened by a wish. The plea is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/14-a-row-of-proverbs/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 14</a>.
