## Where it comes from

A peg — about three wedges, driven in like the thing it draws.
Its great reading is *du₃*, "to build": the verb of every temple
raised in this school's readings, and of the foundation pegs
that builders actually hammered into new walls as the gods'
receipt.

## How to remember it

**Build** starts with one peg in the ground: stake the corner,
raise the wall. The sign IS the first act of construction — read
it and hear the mallet.

Piece by piece:

- **e₂** — a house
- **lu₂ zid-da** — by a true man
- **du₃-a** — built: the peg-verb, wrapped into a description

The proverb trusts the honest builder — and the peg-sign does
the building. The line is read in
