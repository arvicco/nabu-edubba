## Where it comes from

Two working readings: as *niŋ₂* it is "thing, property" — the
thing-maker prefix, doing for concrete stuff what *nam-* does
for abstractions (*niŋ₂-ba*, "a thing allotted": a gift). As
*gar* it is "to place, to set." A ration or portion by origin —
the GAR inside
[KA×GAR]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/kaxgar/' | relative_url }})
is this sign, being eaten.

## How to remember it

A **thing** is whatever can be set down on the table — and this
sign does both: names the stuff (*niŋ₂*) and sets it in place
(*gar*). If you can stack it, count it, or gift it, it starts
here.
