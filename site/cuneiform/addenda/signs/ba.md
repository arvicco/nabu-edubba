## Where it comes from

Pictorial origin opaque — a pure workhorse, top six in both
counted corpora. On receipts it is a whole verb: *ba*, "allotted,
shared out." Inside a verb chain it is the prefix *ba-*,
coloring the action toward "away, for itself." Same wedges,
different seat — the difference is position, not the sign.

## How to remember it

To **allot** is to break the loaf: the sign splits into a few
quick wedges like portions off a block. Rations, fields, fates —
whatever the archive hands out, this sign does the handing.

Piece by piece:

- **i₃-** — the chain's neutral opener
- **-na-** — to him
- **ba** — allotted: this sign as the stem itself

One verb chain, complete: opener, beneficiary, and the
sharing-out at its heart. It is unpacked in
