## Where it comes from

A human head in profile — one of the classic demonstration pieces
of the script's history, because you can watch it turn: the early
drawing lies on its back after the script's quarter-rotation, and
the wedge-built late form still keeps the jaw and skull-line. It
writes *saŋ*, "head," and by extension "first, foremost."

## How to remember it

A **head** in profile, tipped back: forehead, jaw, the neck-line
under it. Whatever is *saŋ* comes first — the head of a line, the
head of a household, the front of anything.

Piece by piece:

- **ur-saŋ** — hero: dog-of-the-head, the one out front
- **zu / nu-zu** — known / not known, the negation flipping it

Gilgameš's epic counts its heroes, famous and nameless, and *saŋ*
makes them heroes — the ones at the head. The line is read in
