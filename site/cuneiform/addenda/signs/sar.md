## Where it comes from

A garden plot in origin, the sign lists say — planted rows,
which is also a fair description of good handwriting. It writes
*sar*, "to write," and with the tablet-sign makes the script's
proudest title: *dub-sar*, tablet-writer — the scribe.

## How to remember it

To **write** is to garden in clay: set your rows straight, keep
the beds even, harvest meaning. Every scribe who signed
*dub-sar* was calling himself a planter of lines.
