## Where it comes from

The picture behind it is not transparent in the late form — the
sign lists offer no confident story. Its power is in what it
builds: *gal* "big, great" makes the palace (*e₂-gal*, "big
house"), and fused with [LU₂]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/lu2/' | relative_url }})
"man" it makes [LUGAL]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/lugal/' | relative_url }})
— the king, literally the *big man*.

## How to remember it

**Big** things get the big open wedges: the sign spreads wide like
arms flung out to show *this big*. Whatever stands next to it —
house, man, mountain — has just been enlarged.

Piece by piece:

- **lugal** — king
- **gal** — greatness, big
- **zu** — to know

A person's name that is a whole sentence — and *gal* twice over,
once fused inside *lugal*, once on its own. The name is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/06-names-that-are-sentences/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 06</a>.
