## Where it comes from

Look closely: it is the day-sign
[𒌓]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/ud/' | relative_url }}) with a
thirty tucked inside — a month IS thirty days, drawn as exactly
that. The workhorse of every dated receipt: *iti* such-and-such,
"month of …," names the slot in the civil calendar.

## How to remember it

A **month** is a box of days: the day-sign made into a
container, the number thirty rattling inside. When a tablet
needs a date, it reaches for the box first, then names which
box.

- **iti** — month: the box of days
- **a₂-ki-ti** — of the Akiti, Ur's great festival

A bookkeeper dates a delivery by the festival everyone knew.
