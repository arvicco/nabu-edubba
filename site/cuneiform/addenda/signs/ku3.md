## Where it comes from

*Ku₃* is "pure, holy, shining" — and silver is "pure metal" in
Sumerian long before it is money, so the same sign prices goods
and blesses temples. Royal praise leans on it constantly: pure
house, pure hand, pure rites. The fuller spoken shape is *kug*,
its final consonant surfacing before vowels.

## How to remember it

**Pure** is what gleams: five clean wedges, nothing extra — the
sign itself kept spotless. Temple or silver ingot, if it
glitters without tarnish, the scribes reached for this sign.
