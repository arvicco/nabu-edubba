## Where it comes from

The homecoming verb: *gi₄* is "to return, to restore" — and,
since a reply is a message coming home, "to answer." Hearts are
restored to their places with it, messengers turn around on it,
and doubled (*gi₄-gi₄*) the returning keeps happening.

## How to remember it

**Return** is the reed-sign's round trip: it looks like its
lookalike [GI]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/gi/' | relative_url }})
the reed, but with more to carry — the sign that goes out comes
back loaded. Answer, homecoming, restoration: all the same
journey home.

Piece by piece:

- **šag₄-zu ki-bi** — your heart, to its place
- **ha-ma-** — may it, for me
- **gi₄-gi₄** — return: the homecoming, doubled to make sure

The standard consolation of the hymns — anger goes home the way
messengers do. The plea is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/14-a-row-of-proverbs/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 14</a>.
