## Where it comes from

Three wedges — among the simplest signs of the whole Sumerian
course — and pure function: *nu* is the negation. Set before a
verb it flips the sentence flat: *zu* "known," *nu-zu* "unknown";
*ak* "done," *nu-ak* "not done."

## How to remember it

**Not** is a small bar across the door: three strokes, no
ceremony, entry denied. The smallest signs carry the largest
switches — this one turns any statement off.
