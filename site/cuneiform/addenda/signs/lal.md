## Where it comes from

*La₂* is "to hang, to weigh out" — the scales' verb on the
receipts, where silver hangs in the balance; in account notation
it even means "minus." As a spelling it is the glue inside
grand names: *{d}en-lil₂-la₂*, "of Enlil," as the refrains
write it.

## How to remember it

**Weigh**: two pans hanging from a beam — the sign suspends its
wedges the same way. Whatever hangs is being measured; whatever
is *la₂* on a tablet has been put on the scale.

Piece by piece:

- **šag₄** — the heart
- **{d}en-lil₂-la₂** — of Enlil: this sign closing the god's
  genitive
- **gu₂-bi nam-gi₄** — overflowed its banks

The scales-sign, spelling the possessive of the god who weighs
all fates. The line is read in
