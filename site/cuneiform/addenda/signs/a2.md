## Where it comes from

*A₂* is the arm — and everything an arm stands for: strength,
labor, a side. With
[zi]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/zi/' | relative_url }}) it
makes *a₂ zid-da*, "the right side," literally "the true arm."
Festivals, wages, and directions all borrow the limb.

## How to remember it

An **arm** bent at the elbow — the wedge angles like a bicep at
work. Strength, wages ("arm-hire"), the right-hand side: when
the body politic needs a limb, this is the one it raises.

Piece by piece:

- **iti** — month
- **a₂-ki-ti** — the Akiti festival, its name opening on the
  arm-sign

The great new-year festival of Ur, dated into a bookkeeper's
line. The month is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/13-you-are/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 13</a>.
