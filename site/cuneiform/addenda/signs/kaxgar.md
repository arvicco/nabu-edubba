## Where it comes from

[KA]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/ka/' | relative_url }}) the
mouth with GAR — a ration, a portion — written inside it: KA×GAR
in the sign lists' notation. A portion in the mouth is eating: the
compound reads *gu₇*, "to eat," the everyday verb of ration lists
and proverbs alike, mid-table in both counted corpora.

## How to remember it

**Eat**: the mouth with its portion already in. Same staging as
its twin [drink]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/kaxa/' | relative_url }})
— only the filling differs: streams of water to drink, a packed
ration to eat. Check what is in the mouth before you read it.

well-fed sibling of
