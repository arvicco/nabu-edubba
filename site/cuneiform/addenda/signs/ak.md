## Where it comes from

Origin unclear — no picture worth teaching. The word is the
plainest verb Sumerian has: *ak*, "to do, to make." By a pure
accident of sound it matches the genitive ending */-ak/*, "of" —
which is why the course teaches the sign and the case in the same
breath.

## How to remember it

**Do** is a hammer-tap of a sign: short, blunt, six wedges of
getting-it-done. And keep the pun: *ak* the deed, *-ak* the "of"
— the doing and the belonging share one sound.

Piece by piece:

- **niŋ₂ ak-zu** — the things of your doing
- **a-ba** — who?
- **i₃-zu-zu** — can ever know them

A god's deeds, beyond counting — and *ak* is the word for the
doing. The line is read in
