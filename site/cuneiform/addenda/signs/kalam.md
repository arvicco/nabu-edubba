## Where it comes from

Not "a land" among others but **the** Land — the way a Sumerian
text names its own country, as against the foreign
[kur]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/kur/' | relative_url }}) on
the horizon. Kings are *lugal kalam-ma*, "king of the Land";
goddesses are its mother.

## How to remember it

**The Land** is home ground: where the mountain-sign looks
outward at the highlands, this sign plants itself in the river
plain and means *ours*. One word of geography that is really a
word of belonging.
