## Where it comes from

*Tu*, fully *tu-ud*: "to be born, to give birth." The royal
hymns run on it — kings in that genre are always being born
magnificently, of goddesses, of dragons, since the day itself.
Everyone else is simply born, with the same sign.

## How to remember it

**Born**: the smallest door in the script, and everyone comes
through it. When a hymn wants a hero's origin story, this sign
opens the account — *since I was born…*

Piece by piece:

- **ki … -ta** — "from (the place of) …": place plus the ablative,
  the standard transfer formula
- **a-tu** — the man's name, Atu — this sign closing it

Goods change hands, and the record names their source. The hymn
where this sign does its birth-work is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/15-i-am-the-king/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 15</a>.
