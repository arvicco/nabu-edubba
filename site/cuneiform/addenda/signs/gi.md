## Where it comes from

The reed — the writing tool itself, finally a sign of its own:
the stalk that, cut to a stylus, pressed every wedge you have
read. As a syllable it serves in names; most famously as the
*gi* of Šul-gi, the great king of Ur.

## How to remember it

The **reed** is the pen drawing its own portrait: one stalk,
leaves flagging off it. Every sign in this codex was pressed by
a reed — this is the sign where the tool signs its work.

Piece by piece:

- **{d}šul-gi-me-en** — I am Šulgi: the reed closing the royal
  name
- **ba-tu-ud-de₃-en-na-ta** — since I was born
- **nitah kalag-ga-me-en** — a mighty male am I

A king whose name ends in a reed — fitting, for the king the
schools remembered as their patron. The hymn is read in
<a href="{{ '/cuneiform/102/15-i-am-the-king/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 15</a>.
