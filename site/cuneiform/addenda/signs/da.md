## Where it comes from

An arm or side, by the usual reading of the picture. As a word,
*da* is the side of something; as a grammar tag, *-da* is the
comitative — "together with": *dumu-da*, "with the child." What
stands at your side comes along with you.

## How to remember it

Your **side** is where company walks: the sign spreads a
shoulder-and-arm of wedges, making room for whoever comes along.
See *-da*, add "with."

Piece by piece:

- **lugal** — the king
- **a₂-zi-da** — the right side, literally "the true arm," this
  sign closing the phrase

A name that leans a whole life on the king's strong side. The
name is read in
