## Where it comes from

Its course-taught reading is *šul*: the young man in his prime,
the hero-word of the hymns — and the first syllable of Šul-gi,
"noble youth," the king of Ur whose praise poems the tablet
houses copied for centuries.

## How to remember it

The **hero** is the youth at full strength — the word kings kept
for themselves even when gray. Read *šul* and picture the prime
of life, wearing a crown it claimed early.
