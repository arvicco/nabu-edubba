## Where it comes from

The closing verb of the votive genre: *a mu-na-ru*, "dedicated
this to him" — the last words of a thousand gifts to the gods,
carved on bowls, statues, and door-sockets. Where the gift ends,
this sign stands.

## How to remember it

To **dedicate** is to let go: the sign leans forward like hands
releasing an offering onto the altar. When you read *…ru* at the
end of an inscription, the object you are holding was itself the
gift.

Piece by piece:

- **a** — the object slot, compressed to a vowel
- **mu-na-** — did, for him
- **ru** — dedicate: the genre's final act
