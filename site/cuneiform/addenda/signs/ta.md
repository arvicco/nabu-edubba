## Where it comes from

Pictorial origin not transparent — a grammar workhorse from the
start. Hung on the end of a noun, *-ta* is the ablative: "from,
out of, since." Wherever a text says where something came from or
when something started counting, this sign closes the phrase.

## How to remember it

**From** points away: the sign's long wedge pushes off its
cluster like a departure. Read *-ta* and ask *from where? since
when?* — the answer is the word it rides on.

Piece by piece:

- **iti** — month
- **a₂-ki-ti** — of the Akiti festival
- **-ta** — from: the bookkeeper counting time forward from a
  festival

An accountant's "since": rations owed from Akiti onward. The line
is read in
