## Where it comes from

The syllable *in* — above all as the verbal prefix *in-*, the
plain "he/she/it did" opener of thousands of administrative and
literary lines alike. No picture to lean on; its whole story is
the front seat of the verb.

## How to remember it

**In-** gets the verb going the way a key turns a lock: the
sentence's doing starts the moment you read it. Where the chain
opens with these wedges, someone has already acted.

Piece by piece:

- **ki** — the place
- **ur-mah-e** — the lion (literally "great dog"), marked as doer
- **lu₂** — a man
- **in-gu₇-a** — ate: this sign opening the verb, *-a* wrapping
  the clause into "where"

A proverb's scene of the crime, and the verb starts on *in-*.
