## Where it comes from

*Gu₂* is the neck — and, by the land's own anatomy, the
riverbank: the raised rim a flood must top. In accounts it also
serves as "total," the sum a column's entries pile up to.
Whatever rises past the rim — water, feeling, figures — has
crossed this sign.

## How to remember it

A **neck** is where overflow happens: the river's bank is its
neck, and a heart in flood "overflows its banks." Collars,
brinks, and totals — everything that rims a fullness wears this
sign.

Piece by piece:

- **šag₄** — the heart
- **gu₂-bi** — its banks, literally "its neck"
- **nam-gi₄** — overflowed
