## Where it comes from

The stated identity is the dog — the working animal of the
streets and the proverbs. In names it turns respectful: *ur-* plus
a god or a king means roughly "servant (hound) of" — Ur-Nanše,
Ur-Namma, half the rosters of Ur III — the loyal dog of someone
worth following.

## How to remember it

A **dog** at heel: compact, alert, five quick wedges. In a proverb
it wanders and steals; at the front of a name it sits obediently
before its master — same dog, two moods.
