## Where it comes from

Early forms are read as a tablet shown with its ruled lines — the
writing surface drawn as its own sign, though the late shape has
grown opaque. It writes *dub*, "(clay) tablet," and builds the
script's proudest title: *dub-sar*, "tablet-writer" — the scribe.

## How to remember it

A **tablet** with its ruling still visible: a slab crossed by the
lines a scribe pressed to keep the writing straight. The script's
own self-portrait — the thing you are reading, drawn inside
itself.
