## Where it comes from

Its pictorial past is opaque — the late form keeps no legible
figure, and the sign lists tell no story worth repeating. Its work
is everywhere regardless: *lu₂* is "man, person," the generic
human of laws, proverbs, and job titles, and the LU₂ inside
[LUGAL]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/lugal/' | relative_url }})
is this very sign, grown big.

## How to remember it

A **man** standing at his plot: the wedges square off a little
frame — one person's standing room. Every proverb's "a man…," every
roster's "person who…" begins here; add *big* and he becomes the
king.

Piece by piece:

- **lu₂** — the man
- **šu** — hand
- **ba-ti** — took to himself: *ba-* coloring the verb toward the
  receiver, *ti* "approach"

The star sentence of the whole receipts genre: a man, a hand, a
taking. It is assembled in
