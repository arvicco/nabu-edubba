## Where it comes from

Five wedges for one small sound: the bare vowel *i*. It spells
names and professions, and doubled it writes the verb "to bring
forth, to proclaim." The laments' own wail, *i-lu*, opens on it
— the script's plainest cry.

## How to remember it

A **cry** is the smallest word there is — one vowel, thrown into
the air. Five wedges stacked like the breath behind a shout;
when grief or proclamation needs a sound before it needs a word,
this is the sign.
