## Where it comes from

*Ama* is mother — in the rosters a parent, in the hymns a title
of the great goddesses: *ama kalam-ma*, "mother of the Land";
*ama diŋir-re-ne*, "mother of the gods." A word so old it sounds
the same in half the world's nurseries.

## How to remember it

**Mother** is the word children everywhere say first — *ama* —
and the sign keeps it: a star-marked enclosure, the household
with its hearth. Where a goddess mothers a whole country, this
sign carries the title.

- **ama** — mother
- **diŋir-re-ne** — of the gods
- **-ra** — for her
