## Where it comes from

A picture that stayed honest: forearm and fingers, still legible
after two thousand years of wedge-building. *Šu* is the hand —
and the hand is how things change owners, so the receipts' key
verb is *šu … ti*, "the hand made it approach": received.

## How to remember it

A **hand** held out, fingers spread — the wedges fan like
knuckles. Everything given, taken, or signed for in the archives
passes through this sign; follow the hand and you follow the
goods.

- **šu** — hand
- **i** — the bare vowel finishing the word
