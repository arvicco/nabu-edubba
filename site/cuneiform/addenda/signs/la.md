## Where it comes from

As a word, abundance (stated); as a working syllable, the
spelling glue of the corpus — *la* smooths joins inside words
and formulas: *nam-ti-la-ni-še₃*, "for his life"; *lu₂-kal-la*,
"the precious one." Plenty of everything, including work.

## How to remember it

**Abundance** overflows: nine wedges heaped like grain over the
rim. And like plenty itself, the sign turns up everywhere —
wherever two syllables need a generous join, *la* fills the gap.

- **lu₂** — man
- **kal** — precious
- **-la** — this sign, rounding the name off
