## Where it comes from

In origin a vessel (stated). In grammar it is *-bi*: "its, that"
— the possessive of THINGS and places, as against persons, who
take *-ani*. A canal's banks, a house's wall, a story's ending:
all wear *-bi*.

## How to remember it

A jug on a shelf, and you ask whose lid that is: **its** — the
jug's own. Things own things through this sign; people go
another way. If the owner could not sign a receipt, the
possessive is *-bi*.

Piece by piece:

- **ša₃-bi** — its heart, its inside
- **nu-zu** — not known

The interior of a god, owned by the god the way a jar owns its
lid — and closed. The line is read in
