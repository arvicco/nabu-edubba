## Where it comes from

Four wedges, several jobs: as *dab₅* it is "to seize" — the hand
of fate, the bailiff's grip, the taking-charge of workers on a
roster. As *tuš* it is "to sit, to dwell." Seizing and settling:
what the hand closes on, someone ends up sitting on.

## How to remember it

**Seize**: a clamp of wedges, closing. When a god seizes a
glutton or an officer seizes a work-gang, the script uses this
grip — and once the seizing is done, the same sign sits down to
stay.
