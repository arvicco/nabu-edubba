## Where it comes from

Commonly explained as a building or walled enclosure, though the
late form has grown opaque. It writes *e₂*, "house" — and a god's
house is a temple, so most of the great sanctuaries are *e₂*-names:
E-kur, "Mountain House"; *e₂-gal*, "big house," the palace. It is
the É of *é-dub-ba-a*, the tablet house — this school.

## How to remember it

A **house** in cross-section: tall frame, floors barred across,
one of the most wedge-heavy signs you own — because a house takes
work to build. If a word starts with this sign, someone lives or
worships inside it.
