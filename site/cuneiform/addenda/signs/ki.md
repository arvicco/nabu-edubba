## Where it comes from

Its pictorial origin is genuinely debated — the scholarship offers
no story worth teaching, so learn the shape: a diamond with a
barred heart. What is certain is the meaning: *ki* is earth,
ground, place — the counterpart of [AN]({{ '/cuneiform/addenda/signs/an/' | relative_url }})'s
heaven — and, written silently after a city or land, the
determinative *{ki}*: *this word was a place*.

## How to remember it

A **place** on a map: a plot of ground marked out with its
boundary lines drawn through it — a surveyor's diamond. Where the
star means the world above, this fenced plot means the ground you
stand on.

Piece by piece:

- **lugal** — king
- **uri₅** — Ur, its sign still boxed for you
- **{ki}** — the silent tag: *that was a city*

A royal seal names the city, and the place-tag seals the claim.
