# Learn to read the world's writing

**Edubba** (Sumerian *é-dub-ba-a*, "the tablet house" — the scribal
school of ancient Mesopotamia) is a free, self-paced school of writing
systems. Each **school** below teaches one scripting tradition through
a catalog of progressive courses: from how the system works, through
your first signs, to reading real tablets, inscriptions, and pages —
drawn from the [Nabu](https://arvicco.github.io/nabu/) library and
cited to the exact source.

No apps, no accounts, no scripts: plain pages you can read anywhere,
at any pace, forever.

## The family tree of writing

Writing was invented from scratch only a handful of times. Almost
every script you have ever seen descends from one of these
inventions — by adoption, adaptation, or inspiration:

<ul class="tree">
  <li><strong>Mesopotamia</strong> — cuneiform (Sumerian), adapted for
    Akkadian, Elamite, Hittite, Hurrian and Urartian, Ugaritic, Old
    Persian — extinct, deciphered
  </li>
  <li><strong>Egypt</strong> — hieroglyphs, with cursive hieratic and
    demotic; alphabetic experiments in its shadow led to
    <ul>
      <li>Proto-Sinaitic → Phoenician, ancestor of
        <ul>
          <li>Greek → Latin, Cyrillic, and most of Europe's scripts</li>
          <li>Aramaic → Hebrew and Arabic scripts, and (most scholars
            hold) Brahmi → Devanagari and the scripts of South and
            Southeast Asia</li>
          <li>runes and ogham, at the family's far northwestern edge</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><strong>China</strong> — hanzi, continuously written for over
    three millennia; adopted and adapted as Japanese kanji (with the
    kana syllabaries) and historically across East Asia
  </li>
  <li><strong>Mesoamerica</strong> — Maya glyphs, the New World's
    fully-fledged writing, deciphered within living memory
  </li>
</ul>

A few scripts still guard their secrets — the Indus signs, Linear A,
Rongorongo. A future school will be about exactly that: how
decipherment works, and why these have not yielded yet.
