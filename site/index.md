# The tablet house is being built

**Edubba** (Sumerian *é-dub-ba-a*, "the tablet house") will be a free,
self-paced school of writing systems: schools for the world's great
scripting traditions — cuneiform first, then Egyptian hieroglyphs,
Hanzi and Kanji, and more — each a catalog of courses leading from
first signs to genuine reading literacy, capped by real tablets,
inscriptions, and pages from the [Nabu](https://arvicco.github.io/nabu/)
library.

Nothing is here yet: this page is the construction sign. The first
courses of the cuneiform school are being written now.

Corrections and suggestions are welcome in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/arvicco/nabu-edubba/issues).
