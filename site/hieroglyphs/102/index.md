# Hieroglyphs 102 · Middle Egyptian

**The literacy track.** Prerequisite:
[101 · Foundations]({{ '/hieroglyphs/101/' | relative_url }}) — this
course assumes its 53 signs, its conventions, and its habits. Where
101 taught how the script works, 102 is about *mileage*: the sign
inventory grows chapter by chapter in an order computed from corpus
frequency, each chapter adds exactly as much Middle Egyptian grammar
as its readings demand, and the readings are real lines — tales,
medical prescriptions, temple walls — in full hieroglyphs.
