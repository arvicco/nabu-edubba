# Drills — every sign, three ways

Every sign the courses teach, as cards: click one, answer out
loud (or on paper — better), then unfold and check. Each sign
comes three ways — *read it*, *write it* (draw before you peek),
*what does it mean* — and silent classifiers ask for their
function instead of a sound. The deck is interleaved so
neighboring cards never come from the same chapter: harder on
purpose, because mixed practice is what sticks. Every sign's full
story lives one click away on the
[Signs shelf]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/' | relative_url }}).

To drill on paper, print this page: the cards become cut-out
slips (fronts only — the Signs shelf is your answer sheet), ready
for a shoebox Leitner system: three boxes, daily / every few
days / weekly, cards moving up when you get them right and back
to daily when you miss.

## The deck

The order below is today's deal: it reshuffles with the
calendar, so a visit tomorrow replays nothing. Sitting down for
a real session? Cut the deck yourself — every tile is the whole
deck in a different order, and tiles you have already worked
through fade. Pick blind; your hand is the shuffle.
