# Easily confused — side by side

Some signs blur precisely because you know both: each alone is
easy, and the pair is the problem. The cure is to look at them
together, where telling them apart is the whole exercise. Take a
row below, say what separates its signs — a tail's shape, a
basket's mirror, the bend of an arm — then check yourself: either
sign opens its full story on the Signs shelf. When
[the deck]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/drills/' | relative_url }})
deals you one of these and you hesitate, this page is where to
come back to.

The lookalikes, deliberately together: the swallow and the
sparrow, the basket and its mirror, the three arms.
