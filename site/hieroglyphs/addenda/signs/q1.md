## Where it comes from

A block throne in profile — the squared seat of gods and kings.
It writes *st*, "seat, place": where someone belongs is their
*s,t*. And it is the first sign of *Wsjr*, Osiris — throne, then
[eye]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/d4/' | relative_url }}) —
the god whose name begins with where he sits.

## How to remember it

The **throne** fixes a place: to have a seat is to have a
station in the world. When the tale says the prince is "in his
place," the throne-sign is the place.
