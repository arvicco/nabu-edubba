## Where it comes from

A wooden column, drawn lying on its side — architecture's
biggest single timber, flattened to fit the line. It writes
*ꜥꜣ*, "great, big" — great of size: the great house (*pr-ꜥꜣ*,
pharaoh), the great name, the great door.

## How to remember it

**Great** is a column you could not lift — laid down so you can
measure it. Where rank is meant, the
[swallow]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/g36/' | relative_url }})
flies instead; where sheer size is meant, the column lies here.

Piece by piece:

- **Nfr** — the man's name, "Beautiful"
- **rn-ꜥꜣ≡f** — his great name: this column, certifying the
  formal one

Egyptians had big names and everyday names — the column marks
the official article. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/06-life-prosperity-health/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 06</a>.
