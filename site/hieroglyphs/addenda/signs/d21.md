## Where it comes from

A mouth, seen from the front — lips parted, the classic
identification. It writes *r*, and as a word of its own it IS
*rʾ*: mouth, utterance, spell. The preposition *r*, "to, toward,"
rides on the same sound: where the mouth points, the sentence
goes.

## How to remember it

The **mouth** is an open oval — the shape your own lips make
saying *r*. Everything spoken in this script starts here: the
spell is a "mouth," and the mouth writes itself.

Piece by piece:

- **rʾ** — this sign as a whole word: spell, utterance, mouth —
  with the stroke saying *read it as what it shows*
- **n mw** — of water

A book of spells calls each one a "mouth" — speech with power in
it. The sign is taught in
