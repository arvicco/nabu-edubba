## Where it comes from

A fuller's club — the laundry beater that pounded cloth clean.
It writes *ḥm*: on one hand "Majesty" (*ḥm≡f*, "His Majesty"),
on the other "servant" (*ḥm-nṯr*, the god's servant, a priest).
One humble tool, serving both ends of the hierarchy.

## How to remember it

**Majesty** is written with a laundry club — remember the
mismatch and you have the sign forever. The king and the washer
share a syllable; the storytellers' "His Majesty" swings this
club in every tale.
