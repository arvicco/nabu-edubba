## Where it comes from

A milk jug in its carrying net — the vessel slung for the road.
It writes *mj*, "like, as": the preposition of likeness that
lets Egyptian compare a king to the sun and mean it.

## How to remember it

**Likeness** travels in the milk jug: milk takes the shape of
whatever carries it, and *mj* pours one thing into another's
shape. "You, like Aten" — the jug does the pouring.

Piece by piece:

- **msi̯≡tw≡k** — you are born
- **mj** — like: the jug, pouring king into sun
- **msi̯.w Jtn** — the born-ness of Aten

The comparison that made a king a sunrise, hinged on one jug of
milk. The sign is taught in
