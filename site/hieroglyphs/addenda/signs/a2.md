## Where it comes from

The seated man again — but with his hand raised to his mouth. A
silent classifier for everything the mouth does and everything
that feels like it: eat, drink, speak, love, think, keep silent.

## How to remember it

**Hand to mouth** covers every traffic through the lips — food
in, words out, and the thoughts behind both. If a word wears
this figure, something is happening at the mouth or in the mind
behind it.

Piece by piece:

- **swr≡k** — may you drink: water pouring, and the hand at the
  mouth doing the drinking
- **jm≡sn** — from them: the cool waters just promised

A blessing for the dead — drink freely — sealed by the figure
with his hand raised. The classifier is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/09-the-silent-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 09</a>;
Wenamun's drink is poured in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/06-life-prosperity-health/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 06</a>.
