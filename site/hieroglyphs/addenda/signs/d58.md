## Where it comes from

A lower leg with its foot — shin, heel, and toes in profile,
standing. It writes *b*, and unlike the walking pair
[D54]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/d54/' | relative_url }})
this leg goes nowhere: it is planted, a support.

## How to remember it

One **leg**, standing its ground — *b* as in *bear the weight*.
Two legs walk; one leg holds. When negation needs a firm stance
(*bn*, "not"), this is the leg it stands on.

Piece by piece:

- **j:ḫr** — fallen, the striking-man classifier landing the blow
- **sbn** — slides away, this leg inside the slipping

The enemy falls and slides; the leg-sign, ironically, is in the
sliding. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/04-your-first-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 04</a>.
