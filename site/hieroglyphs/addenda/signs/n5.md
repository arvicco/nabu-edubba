## Where it comes from

The sun disc — a circle with its center marked. It writes *rꜥ*,
the sun and the sun-god Ra, and silently classifies days, hours,
and everything the sun governs. In Akhenaten's city the same
disc, as *jtn*, became the whole of god.

## How to remember it

The **sun** needs one circle — the simplest drawing of the most
worshipped thing in Egypt. When time or divinity enters a word,
the disc rises at its end.

Piece by piece:

- **jtn≡f** — his Aten, the disc closing the god's name
- **jtn≡k** — your Aten: the same sun, claimed twice

Two pronouns fighting over one sun — Amarna theology in four
words. The sign is taught in
