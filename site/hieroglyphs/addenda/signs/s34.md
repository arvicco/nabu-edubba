## Where it comes from

The most famous sign in the script, and honestly uncertain in
origin — a sandal strap? a knot? The identification is debated;
what is certain is the word: *ꜥnḫ*, "life, to live" — the loop
every god in every relief holds out to the king's face.

## How to remember it

**Life** is the sign you already knew before you could read: the
ankh. Loop, bar, and stem — whatever it once drew, forty
centuries agreed on what it gives.

Piece by piece:

- **ꜥnḫ≡f** — may he live: the ankh with its complements
- **m ꜥnḫ Rꜥw** — on the life of Ra — the ankh again, feeding a
  god
- **jm** — therefrom

Life twice in one blessing, for man and sun alike. The sign is
