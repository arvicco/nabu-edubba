## Where it comes from

A single grain — a small circle, often written in threes.
Silent: it classifies sand, minerals, powders, pellets, drugs —
anything that comes in grains. The pharmacists' favorite sign.

## How to remember it

One dot of **sand** stands for everything you measure by the
pinch: ochre, incense, salt, medicine. When a recipe's word ends
in the little grain, you could pour the substance through your
fingers.

Piece by piece:

- **jri̯ m** — make into: the doing eye, the owl of "into"
- **pns,t** — a pellet: and the grain-classifier says what kind
  of thing a pellet is
