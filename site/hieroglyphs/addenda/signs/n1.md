## Where it comes from

The sky drawn as the Egyptians built it: a flat slab with its
two ends turned down, the ceiling of the world resting on its
supports. It writes *p,t*, "sky, heaven," and roofs words about
what is above.

## How to remember it

The **sky** is a ceiling — Egypt's cosmos was architecture, and
this is its lintel. Everything under discussion happens under
this slab; gods walk on top of it.

Piece by piece:

- **wnn p,t** — while the sky endures: the slab, holding
- **wnn≡k** — you endure with it

The strongest promise Egyptian can make hangs from this ceiling.
