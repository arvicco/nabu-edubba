## Where it comes from

An eye, drawn whole with its lid-line and inner corner. It is
*jr,t*, "eye," and writes *jr* — above all the verb *jri̯*, "to
do, to make," Egyptian's busiest verb. It also opens the god's
name *Wsjr*, Osiris: throne first, then this eye.

## How to remember it

The **eye** oversees the doing: in Egypt what the eye watches
gets done, and the same sign carries both. See it, and read
either sight or work — the two were never far apart.
