## Where it comes from

The cursive shorthand of the quail chick
[G43]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/g43/' | relative_url }}): a
small spiral, all that survives of the bird when the hand is
hurrying. Same sound *w*, two shapes — the scribes swapped freely
between the drawn bird and the quick coil.

## How to remember it

The **coil** is a chick drawn by a scribe with no time: body,
beak, and feet collapsed into one curl. When you meet the spiral,
hear the same *w* — the bird is in there, compressed.

Piece by piece:

- **mw≡n** — our water, the plural strokes carrying "our"
- **pw** — it is: the coil riding behind *p* in the little
  is-word

The nominal sentence you met in the water chapter — with the
coil, not the chick, closing it. The twins are told apart in
