## Where it comes from

A loaf of bread set on a reed mat — the minimal offering, drawn
whole. It writes *ḥtp*: offering, peace, contentment, rest — the
family of meanings that flows from a table properly laid. It
opens the most copied phrase in Egyptian:
*ḥtp-ḏi̯-nswt*, "an offering which the king gives."

## How to remember it

An **offering** is bread on a mat — nothing more is needed, and
the sign never adds more. Where the loaf rests, there is peace:
*ḥtp* is both the gift and the calm it buys.
