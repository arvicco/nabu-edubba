## Where it comes from

Honestly uncertain: a sieve? a placenta? The identification is
debated, and the course teaches the use, not a story. The use is
solid: it writes *ḫ*, the hard rasp of Scottish "loch."

## How to remember it

A **sieve** rasps when you shake it — grain against mesh, the
sound of *ḫ*. Whether or not the Egyptians drew a sieve, the
hatched disc scratches, and that scratch is the letter.

Piece by piece:

- **fsw** — the cook, literally "the one who cooks"
- **Wnḫ,w** — his name, the sieve-sign rasping its middle

A kitchen man, named for eternity on his master's wall — with
the debated disc doing honest phonetic work. The sign is taught
in <a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/04-your-first-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 04</a>.
