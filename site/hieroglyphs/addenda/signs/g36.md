## Where it comes from

A swallow — forked tail, long wings folded to a point. It writes
*wr*, "great" in the sense of rank and seniority: the great one,
the elder, the chief. Size takes the
[column]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/o29/' | relative_url }});
standing takes this bird.

## How to remember it

The **elder** of the air: swallows return each year older and
first — the bird of seniority. Mind its lookalike, the sparrow
of [G37]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/g37/' | relative_url }}):
the swallow's forked tail lifts a word up, the sparrow's round
tail drags it down.

Piece by piece:

- **Rꜥw** — the sun-god
- **-wr** — is great: the swallow, ranking him

A man named for his god's seniority, swallow-certified. The two
greats are told apart in
