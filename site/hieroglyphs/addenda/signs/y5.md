## Where it comes from

A senet game board seen edge-on, its pieces standing in a row.
It writes *mn* — "to remain, be firm, endure" — and sits inside
two of the biggest names in Egypt: the god Amun (*Jmn*) and
every king called Men-something, from Menes on down.

## How to remember it

The **board** holds its pieces steady in their squares — *mn*,
firmly placed. A game that lasted three thousand years is a fair
picture for "endure."

Piece by piece:

- **mn** — this board, with its complement *n*
- **-j** — the reed closing the name

king. The sign is taught in
