## Where it comes from

The [basket]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/v31/' | relative_url }})
with its handle on the other side — a mirror form the scribes
used freely for the same sound *k*. Egyptian writing runs in
both directions, and some signs kept both mirrors even inside a
single direction of text.

## How to remember it

The **mirror basket** is the same basket picked up with the
other hand. One sound, two shapes: if the handle surprises you,
nothing else changes — say *k* and read on.

Piece by piece:

- **m≡k** — behold: the owl with the mirror basket, the
  "look-you" particle
- **wj** — me
- **m ꜥ≡k** — in your hand: the second mirror basket closing the
  line

"Behold" and "your" in one line, both on the turned basket. The
twins are taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/02-his-majesty-says/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 02</a>.
