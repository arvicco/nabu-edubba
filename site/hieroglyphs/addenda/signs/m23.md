## Where it comes from

A sedge plant — the marsh reed that stood as the emblem of Upper
Egypt. It writes *sw*, and its great word is *nswt*, "king (of
Upper Egypt)": He-of-the-Sedge. In everyday grammar the same
*sw* is the little pronoun "he."

## How to remember it

One **sedge** stem, and the whole south of Egypt stands behind
it — the plant that means the king. And when a sentence needs a
light "he," it borrows the royal plant for a commoner's pronoun.
