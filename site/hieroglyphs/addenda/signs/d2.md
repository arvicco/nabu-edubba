## Where it comes from

A human face, front-on — rare in a script that draws almost
everything in profile. It writes *ḥr*: the noun "face" and the
preposition "upon, over, at" — what your face is toward, you are
*ḥr*.

## How to remember it

The **face** looks straight out at you, like the owl — and
"upon" is where a face points. Turn your face to a task and you
are *ḥr* it: the preposition is a gaze.

Piece by piece:

- **mj,tt** — likewise, the same
- **ḥr(,j)** — the one upon: this face, gazing down at
- **mw pw** — the water — the nominal sentence again

A caution about the man on the river — with the face-sign doing
"upon." The sign is taught in
