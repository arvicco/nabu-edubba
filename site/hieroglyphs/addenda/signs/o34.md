## Where it comes from

A door bolt in profile — the horizontal bar that slides across
to lock a door. It writes *z*, which by Middle Egyptian had
merged in sound with *s*; the scribes kept both signs anyway,
the bolt and the
[folded cloth]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/s29/' | relative_url }}),
old spelling habits outliving the ear.

## How to remember it

The **bolt** slides with a buzz — *zzz* — straight across its
door. One long bar, one flat sound; when a word keeps the old
*z*, this is the bar that locks it in.

Piece by piece:

- **sn tꜣ** — kissing the earth: the bolt opening the verb of
  homage
- **n nb-tꜣ,wj** — for the Lord of the Two Lands, the land-sign
  compressed to one strip

Courtiers flat on the ground before the king — and the bolt-sign
starts the bowing. The line was read whole in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 00</a>.
