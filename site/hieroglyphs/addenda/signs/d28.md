## Where it comes from

Two arms raised from the shoulders, palms forward — the classic
writing of the *ka*: the life-force, the double that is born
with a person and must go on being fed after death. Every
offering formula ultimately addresses someone's ka.

## How to remember it

The **ka** lifts both arms — to receive, to embrace, to say
*here I am*. When bread and beer are set out for the dead, these
raised arms are what actually takes them.

Piece by piece:

- **jt(j)-nṯr.pl** — the god's-fathers, a priestly rank
- **ḥr drp** — are offering
- **n kꜣ≡k** — to your ka: the raised arms, receiving

Priests feed the double, exactly as designed. The ka is taught
in <a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/06-the-offering-formula/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 06</a>.
