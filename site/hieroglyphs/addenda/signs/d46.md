## Where it comes from

A hand, flat and open, thumb up — cut off at the wrist, unlike
the whole [forearm]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/d36/' | relative_url }}).
It writes *d*: the firm tap of a sound, matched to the part of
the body that does the tapping.

## How to remember it

The **hand** does — and *d* is the doing sound: *ḏd* "say"
carries it, and every craftsman's caption keeps it busy. Where
the arm reaches, the bare hand works.

Piece by piece:

- **dw** — applying, laying on: the hand-sign doing exactly that
- **sjn** — the clay, marked with the substance-classifier

A workshop caption: a hand, applying — the sign as its own
demonstration. It is taught in
