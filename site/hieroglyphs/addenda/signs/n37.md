## Where it comes from

A pool of water seen from above — the rectangular garden basin
of an estate. It writes *š* ("sh"), and as a word *š* is the
pool itself; in the working world it stretches to a plot, a
worksite — something with edges that one tends.

## How to remember it

A **pool** asks for quiet — *sh* — and the sign is exactly the
calm rectangle you'd hush someone beside. Flat water, flat
sound.

- **jri̯** — do
- **š≡k** — your plot, your task: the pool-sign standing for the
  assigned patch
- **rf** — then! — the nudge
