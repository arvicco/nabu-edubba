## Where it comes from

Two legs caught mid-stride — a classifier, silent: it follows
verbs of motion and tells the reader *this word moves*. Come, go,
enter, hurry, flee: whatever the verb, the little legs walk
after it.

## How to remember it

**Walking** legs at the end of a word are the script's stage
direction: *exit, moving*. If you see them, something just went
somewhere — read back to find out what.

Piece by piece:

- **Wsjr** — Osiris, in a late spelling: throne, sun, and the
  divine falcon
- **ꜥq≡f** — he enters: and there are the legs, walking the verb
  in
- **r≡tn** — against you

A god on the move, and the classifier moves with him. The silent
signs are taught in
