## Where it comes from

The scarab beetle, drawn from above with its ridged wing-case.
It writes *ḫpr*, "to become, come into being, transform" — the
Egyptians watched new beetles climb out of the dung ball and
read it as self-creation. The morning sun, becoming itself, is
Khepri.

## How to remember it

The **scarab** rolls its ball and life comes out of it —
becoming, engineered by a beetle. Every "become," every
transformation, every Khepri-name carries this insect.

Piece by piece:

- **nn ḫpr≡tn** — you shall not become: the scarab, denied
- **nn wn≡tn** — you shall not be: the hare-verb, denied too

The curse aims at becoming itself — no transformation, no
existence. The sign is taught in
