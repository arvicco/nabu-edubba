## Where it comes from

A man seated on the ground, one knee up, one hand forward — the
script's generic human. Silent, it classifies men, names, and
professions; and it stands alone as the suffix *≡j*, "I, me, my"
— the writer pointing at himself.

## How to remember it

The **person** sits at the end of a word like a signature:
*a human is meant here* — and when the text says "I," the little
seated figure IS the speaker, drawn in person.

Piece by piece:

- **ḏd≡j** — shall I speak: the seated man as "I"
- **n m** — to whom?
- **mjn** — today

The refrain of Egyptian literature's loneliest poem — a man with
no one left to talk to, and the seated figure is him. The
classifier is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/09-the-silent-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 09</a>.
