## Where it comes from

The slope of a hill — a rising bank of sand drawn as a tall
curve. It writes *q* (often printed ḳ): the emphatic k, pressed
from further back in the throat than plain
[k]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/v31/' | relative_url }}).

## How to remember it

Climbing a sand **slope** takes effort from deep in the chest —
and so does *q*, the k with weight behind it. Plain k walks on
flat ground; q climbs.

Piece by piece:

- **qr** — heating in a stack: the slope-sign opening the
  bakery's verb
- **bḏꜣ** — the bread-molds

The bakers stack their molds on the fire, and the effortful *q*
starts the work. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/04-your-first-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 04</a>.
