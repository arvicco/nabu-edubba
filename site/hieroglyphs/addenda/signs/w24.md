## Where it comes from

A small round pot — the *nw*-jar of offerings, drawn as a plain
bulb. It writes *nw*, and hides inside common words: the *jnk*
"I am" of self-presentations often carries the little pot in its
spelling.

## How to remember it

The **pot** is round as the mouth saying "nw." Small, common,
easy to overlook — like the grammar it helps spell: the biggest
word it lives in is "I."

Piece by piece:

- **ḥnk** — to offer, to present — the giving arm opening it
- **-t-j** — the name's ending, with this pot in the spelling

A woman named for generosity, her name poured through the little
jar. The sign is taught in
