## Where it comes from

The Egyptian vulture — a real species, *Neophron percnopterus*,
with the naked face and shaggy ruff the sign still draws. It
writes *ꜣ*, the glottal catch conventionally read as "a": the
sound at the start of a cough, carried by a carrion bird.

## How to remember it

The **vulture** opens its beak and what comes out is barely a
sound — *ꜣ*, the catch in the throat. Big bird, smallest of
noises: that mismatch is the memory.

Piece by piece:

- **qr** — heating, stacked in the oven
- **bḏꜣ** — the bread-mold, its vulture carrying the final ꜣ

A bakery scene painted in a tomb, labeled by the painter — the
vulture at work in a word for kitchenware. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/04-your-first-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 04</a>.
