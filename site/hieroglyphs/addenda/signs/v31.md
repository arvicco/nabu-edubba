## Where it comes from

A wicker basket with its handle, seen from the side. It writes
*k*, and above all the suffix pronoun *≡k*, "you, your" (said to
a man) — the basket hanging off the ends of words the way it
hung off an arm.

## How to remember it

The **basket** has a handle because it is meant to be YOURS to
carry — and *≡k* is exactly that: your name, your house, your
heart, each with the little basket hooked on. Its mirror twin
[V31A]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/v31a/' | relative_url }})
faces the other way.
