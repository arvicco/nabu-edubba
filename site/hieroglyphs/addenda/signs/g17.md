## Where it comes from

An owl — the classic identification, and unmistakable: alone
among the script's birds it turns its face to look straight at
you, exactly as owls do. It writes the sound *m*, which makes it
the preposition "in, as, with" and half the grammar of any page.

## How to remember it

The **owl** stares out of the wall at you, and you cannot pass a
line without meeting it — *m* is "in," and the owl is IN
everything. Face-on bird, everywhere: that is *m*.

Piece by piece:

- **m** — the owl as "don't": the negative imperative borrows its
  sound
- **ꜣz** — hurry
