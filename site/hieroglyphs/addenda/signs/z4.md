## Where it comes from

Two short diagonal strokes — no picture, pure notation, the
lightweight way to write *y* (usually at the end of a word),
where the fuller spelling would draw the reed twice. Two marks
for a doubled sound.

## How to remember it

A **pair** of ticks: *y* is *j* twice, and the scribes kept the
arithmetic — two little strokes instead of two whole reeds.
Count the marks, hear the doubling.

Piece by piece:

- **tm** — to be no more, ended — the pair-strokes riding in its
  spelling, the sad sparrow and the bookroll classifying the doom
- **rn≡k** — your name, its death-sign written with the cautious
  substitute stroke

A curse unmakes the enemy by unmaking his name. The stroke-pair
