## Where it comes from

A papyrus roll, tied shut and sealed — a book closed on its
contents. Silent: it classifies abstractions — know, think,
plan, truth, annihilation — everything real that has no shape to
draw. What cannot be pictured is filed as *written*.

## How to remember it

An **idea** lives in a closed book: the scribes' own admission
that some things exist only on paper. When a word wears the tied
roll, the thing it names is thinkable, not touchable.

Piece by piece:

- **jw≡k dr.ṱ** — you are driven off, the striking arm landing
  it
- **dm.ṱ** — annihilated: unmaking is an abstraction, so the
  tied roll files it

Even destruction, being an idea, gets the bookroll. The
classifier is taught in
