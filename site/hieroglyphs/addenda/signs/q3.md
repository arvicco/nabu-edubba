## Where it comes from

A stool or mat of woven reed, seen from above — a plain square
of furniture. It writes *p*; and because the little word *pw*,
"it is," starts with *p*, this humble seat props up half the
definitions in Egyptian literature.

## How to remember it

A **stool** is the simplest thing that holds you up — and *pw*,
"it is," is the simplest sentence that holds a claim up. Square
seat, square sound, load-bearing both.

Piece by piece:

- **mw** — water
- **pw** — it is: the stool-sign, compressed to bare *p* here,
  carrying the whole sentence

Ptahhotep's two-word verdict on the fleeting thing he is
describing — the very line that opened
