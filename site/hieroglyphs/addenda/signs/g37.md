## Where it comes from

A sparrow — round tail, hunched little body. Silent: it
classifies smallness, weakness, badness, ruin. To the granary
culture that fed Egypt, the seed-stealing sparrow was the
fitting mascot for everything going wrong.

## How to remember it

**Smallness** with feathers: where the good gods get falcons,
trouble gets the sparrow. If a word carries this bird, the news
is bad, weak, or shrinking — brace before you translate. Its
lookalike, the swallow of
[G36]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/g36/' | relative_url }}),
means the opposite: watch the tail.

Piece by piece:

- **nn wnn≡k** — you shall not exist: the hare-verb of being,
  refused
- **dm.ṱ** — annihilated: and the sparrow perches on the ruin

The enemy shrinks to nothing, sparrow-marked. The classifier is
