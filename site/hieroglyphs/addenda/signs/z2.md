## Where it comes from

Three short strokes in a row — no picture, pure bookkeeping.
Silent: written under or after a word, they mark it plural.
Three is the script's "many," as it is in most of the world's
tally systems.

## How to remember it

Count the **plural**: one stroke is the thing itself, three
strokes are things beyond counting. When gods, names, or loaves
multiply, these three ticks do the multiplying.

Piece by piece:

- **nn** — shall not be: the arms of refusal
- **rn.pl** — names, pluralized by this sign's three ticks, the
  danger written with the safe substitute stroke
- **≡tn** — your, plural again

A curse takes every name at once — the plural strokes make sure
none escapes. The classifier is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/09-the-silent-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 09</a>.
