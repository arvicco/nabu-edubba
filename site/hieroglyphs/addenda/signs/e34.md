## Where it comes from

A desert hare at full stretch, ears flat along its back. It
writes *wn* — the sound of *wnn*, "to be, to exist," the verb a
verbless language reaches for when being itself needs saying.

## How to remember it

The **hare** exists at speed — blink and it is elsewhere, but it
IS, emphatically. When Egyptian insists that something exists,
endures, will always be, the hare runs through the sentence.

Piece by piece:

- **wnn p,t** — the sky exists: the hare, then heaven
- **wnn≡k** — you exist: the hare again, running the promise
  home
