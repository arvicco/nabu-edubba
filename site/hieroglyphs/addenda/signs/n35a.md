## Where it comes from

Three ripple-lines stacked — one
[ripple]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/n35/' | relative_url }})
tripled into depth. It writes *mw*, "water," and follows liquid
words as their silent classifier: drink, wash, flood, tears —
whatever pours, these three lines pour after it.

## How to remember it

One ripple is a sound; three ripples are **water** you could
fall into. Depth is drawn by stacking — and every liquid in the
language carries this little pond along.

Piece by piece:

- **jꜥi̯** — wash: arm, reed, and the water-sign pouring over
  the verb
- **tw** — yourself

An instruction to bathe — with the water classifier doing the
splashing. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/01-it-is-water/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 01</a>.
