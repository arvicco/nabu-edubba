## Where it comes from

A forearm whose hand grips a stick — the bare
[forearm]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/d36/' | relative_url }})
armed. Silent: it classifies strength and forceful action — the
arm-family's strongman, next to the empty palm and the
loaf-bearing
[giver]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/d37/' | relative_url }}).

## How to remember it

**Force** is an arm plus a lever: the same limb that gives and
carries, now holding a stick. Three arms, three tempers — open,
giving, armed — and this is the armed one.

Piece by piece:

- **bn** — not, the firm leg holding the negation
- **krp≡tw≡f** — shall it be torn out: this armed arm marking
  the violence being forbidden

A boundary stela defends itself: no force shall move it — and
the force in question is drawn into the verb. The sign is taught
in <a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/102/05-another-remedy/' | relative_url }}">102's chapter 05</a>.
