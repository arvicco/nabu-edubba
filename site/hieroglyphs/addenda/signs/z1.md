## Where it comes from

Not a picture at all — a single upright stroke, the script's own
punctuation. Set under or beside a sign it says: *read this one
as the thing it shows*. A mouth with the stroke is a mouth, not
the sound *r*; a house with the stroke is a house.

## How to remember it

The **stroke** is the script pointing at its own picture: "yes,
really, THE THING." One small mark, and a sound-sign turns back
into the object it always drew.

Piece by piece:

- **rʾ** — the mouth-sign, and under it this stroke: read it as
  the word "mouth/spell" itself, not as the sound *r*
- **n mw** — of water

One stroke turns a letter back into a word. The marker is taught
in <a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/03-how-signs-mean/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 03</a>.
