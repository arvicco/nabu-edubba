## Where it comes from

A forearm, palm up — elbow to open hand in one clean line. It
writes *ꜥ*, the deep pharyngeal sound conventionally read as
"a"; as a word, *ꜥ* is also "arm, hand" itself, so the picture
never stopped meaning what it shows.

## How to remember it

The **forearm** reaches out flat — the sound *ꜥ* comes from just
as deep as an arm is long. It waits inside the sun-god's own
name: *Rꜥw*, mouth-arm-sun.

Piece by piece:

- **Rꜥw** — the sun-god's name: mouth, this forearm, then the
  great swallow
- **-wr** — great, of rank

A courtier named after his god's greatness, the forearm holding
the name's middle. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/04-your-first-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 04</a>.
