## Where it comes from

A pustule — probably: the picture is debated, and the course
teaches the use, not a story. Silent: it classifies sickness,
wounds, secretions, and bodily conditions — the flag of the
medical papyri, where hardly a line lacks it.

## How to remember it

**Sickness** is a swelling drawn small: whatever the little
lump once was, the doctors made it their filing mark. In a
remedy, the words wearing it are the complaint and its
discharges — the reason the papyrus was written.

- **dw** — applying: the hand at work
- **sjn** — the clay poultice, wearing this classifier as a
  bodily-substance mark
