## Where it comes from

A shallow wicker basket, no handle — a broad open bowl. It
writes *nb*: "lord, master, owner" — and, by the same sound,
"all, every." Lord of the Two Lands, lord of the sky, every good
thing: the basket underlies them all.

## How to remember it

The **lord** owns whatever the basket holds — and the basket
holds *everything*, which is why the same sign also means
"every." A wide bowl under a word puts that word in someone's
keeping.

Piece by piece:

- **jnk ḥm** — I am a servant
- **sḫpr.n** — whom shaped: the scarab inside the causative
- **nb≡f** — his lord: this basket, with its stroke and his
  viper

A courtier defines himself by his master — the basket-word at
the sentence's heart. The sign is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/07-two-sounds-one-sign/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 07</a>.
