## Where it comes from

Traditionally read as a heart with the windpipe rising from it —
the identification is conventional, not certain. It writes
*nfr*: "good, beautiful, perfect" — the word inside Nefertiti
("the beautiful one has come") and half the flattering names of
Egypt.

## How to remember it

**Beautiful** is a lute-shaped charm of a sign — whatever organ
it drew, it reads as everything Egypt wanted to be: good,
lovely, complete. Queens wore it in their names like jewelry.

Piece by piece:

- **Nfr** — the name: this sign with its complements *f* and *r*
- **rn-ꜥꜣ≡f** — his great name: the "big" column making the name
  official

A man simply named "Beautiful" — and the wall notes it is his
formal name. The sign is taught in
