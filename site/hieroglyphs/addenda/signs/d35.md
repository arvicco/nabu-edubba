## Where it comes from

Two arms spread wide, palms down — the human gesture of refusal
and empty hands, made into a sign. It writes the negations *n*
and *nn*: "not," "there is not." Egyptian says no with its whole
body.

## How to remember it

**Refusal** needs no sound: the open arms that mean "nothing
here, not happening" in any century. When a sentence opens with
this shrug, whatever follows is denied.

Piece by piece:

- **n rdi̯.n≡sn** — they did not put: the refusal-arms flipping
  the giving
- **Jmn** — Amun
- **m jb≡sn** — in their hearts
