## Where it comes from

A fire-drill — the stick-and-socket tool spun to kindle flame.
It writes *ḏꜣ*, the sound at the heart of *wḏꜣ*, "to be whole,
to prosper" — the middle wish of *ꜥnḫ-wḏꜣ-snb*, "life,
prosperity, health" — and of *wḏꜣ*, "to set out, proceed."

## How to remember it

The **fire-drill** spins until the spark takes: prosperity as
friction rewarded. Every "may he prosper" and every "off His
Majesty went" turns this same drill.
