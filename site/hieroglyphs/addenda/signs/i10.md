## Where it comes from

A cobra at rest — hood folded, body looped along the ground. It
writes *ḏ* (the "dj" of "adjust"), and its great word is *ḏd*,
"to say": the verb that opens nearly every speech in Egyptian
narrative.

## How to remember it

The **cobra** speaks first: *ḏd*, "say," begins with it, so
every storyteller's "then His Majesty said…" starts with a
snake. Watch for the coil and hood — someone is about to talk.

Piece by piece:

- **ḏd** — says: this cobra, then the hand
- **ḥm≡f** — His Majesty: the fuller's club with its stroke, the
  viper for "his"

The formula that opens royal speech from the Old Kingdom to the
end — cobra first. The sign is taught in
