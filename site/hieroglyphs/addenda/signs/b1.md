## Where it comes from

A woman seated on the ground, wrapped in her long dress — the
counterpart of the seated
[man]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/a1/' | relative_url }}).
Silent: it classifies women's names and titles, from serving
girls to goddesses.

## How to remember it

The **woman** sits at the close of a name and settles the
question the sounds leave open: *a woman is meant*. Wherever a
mother, daughter, or goddess is written, she sits there in
person.

Piece by piece:

- **Ḥḥ,w** — Huh, the god of endlessness, the falcon marking him
  divine
- **Ḥḥ,t** — Hauhet, his consort: same name, and the seated
  woman makes it hers
- **kꜣ≡tw r-rʾ≡sn** — so one says of them

A god and goddess told apart by one seated figure. The
classifier is taught in
<a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/101/09-the-silent-signs/' | relative_url }}">101's chapter 09</a>.
