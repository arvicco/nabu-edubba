## Where it comes from

An animal's heart with the vessel-stumps still on its shoulders
— anatomy, drawn from the butcher's block. It writes *jb*: the
heart as Egyptians understood it, the seat of thought, intent,
and loyalty — the organ weighed against the feather at the
judgment.

## How to remember it

The **heart** is a jug with two little handles — pick it up and
weigh it, as the gods will. Where we say mind, memory, courage,
Egyptian says *jb*, and draws the organ itself.

- **jb≡sn** — their hearts: the organ with its stroke
- **fn** — grow faint, weak-bird-marked
- **ḥr ḏ,t≡sn** — upon their bodies
