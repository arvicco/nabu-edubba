## Where it comes from

A folded cloth — linen doubled over a stand or arm, hanging in a
tall narrow S of fabric. It writes *s*, one of the commonest
sounds in the language; among its jobs is the causative prefix
*s-*, which turns "live" into "make live."

## How to remember it

The **cloth** hangs in folds that hiss when they slide — *s*.
And put the cloth in front of a verb and it dresses the verb up
into a causing: *ꜥnḫ* live, *s-ꜥnḫ* bring to life.

Piece by piece:

- **s-** — this cloth, the causative: *make…*
- **ꜥnḫ** — …live: the ankh

Two signs, and the sculptor's whole job description: he makes
the stone live. The sign is taught in
