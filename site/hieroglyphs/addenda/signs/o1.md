## Where it comes from

A house in ground-plan: four walls and a gap for the door, drawn
the way an architect would. It writes *pr*, "house" — and *pr-ꜥꜣ*,
the "great house," is the palace, the word that became *pharaoh*.
The same sign lends itself to *pri̯*, "to go out": what a door is
for.

## How to remember it

A **house** seen from above — walls squared, one doorway open.
Everything domestic starts at this plan, and everything that
*goes forth* leaves through its one gap.

Piece by piece:

- **pr≡f** — his house: the floor plan with its word-sign stroke
- **ḫf.y** — destroyed, with the cautious stand-in stroke where a
  dangerous sign would go
