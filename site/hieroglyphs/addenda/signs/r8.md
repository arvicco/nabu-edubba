## Where it comes from

A cloth pennant wound on a tall pole — the flags that flew
before temple gateways. It writes *nṯr*, "god": to see the flag
was to know a god lived there, and the script kept the shorthand.

## How to remember it

The **flag** marks the god's address — you cannot see a god, but
you can see where one is flown. Wherever divinity enters a word,
the temple flag goes up.

Piece by piece:

- **jw≡k m nṯr** — you are a god: the flag with the divine
  falcon
- **pri̯ m nṯr** — come forth from a god: the house-plan door
  doing "forth," the flag again

The dead man is promoted twice in one line, flag after flag. The
sign is taught in
