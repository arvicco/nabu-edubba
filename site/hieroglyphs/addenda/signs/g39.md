## Where it comes from

A pintail duck — long neck, pointed tail, resting. It writes
*zꜣ*, "son" (with the loaf, *zꜣ,t*, "daughter"): the sign of
filiation. Kings are *zꜣ-Rꜥw*, "son of Ra," and every family
wall lines up its children behind this bird.

## How to remember it

The **duck** paddles with its brood behind it — and in the
script the brood is the point: son of, daughter of, child of.
One duck, and a family tree starts.

Piece by piece:

- **zꜣ,t≡f** — his daughter: the duck with the viper
- **S,t≡s-kꜣ,w** — her name, built on throne and basket

A daughter takes her place on her father's wall, led in by the
family duck. The sign is taught in
