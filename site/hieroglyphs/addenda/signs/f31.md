## Where it comes from

Three fox skins tied at the top — the classic identification. It
writes *ms*, the sound of *msi̯*, "to give birth, be born," and
of "born of" in names: Ramesses is "Ra-bore-him," Thutmose
"Thoth-bore-him," each carrying these skins.

## How to remember it

Three **skins** knotted together — offspring bundled to a
mother, the litter in one tie. Every royal name that ends in
"-mose" or "-messes" was born through this sign.

- **msi̯≡tw≡k** — you are born: the skins opening the verb
- **mj msi̯.w Jtn** — like the born-ness of Aten, the skins
  again
