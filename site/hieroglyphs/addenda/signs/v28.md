## Where it comes from

A wick of twisted flax — the lamp-cord, plaited in a spiral. It
writes *ḥ*, the emphatic h pushed from deeper in the throat than
plain [h]({{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/o4/' | relative_url }}).
The twist in the picture matches the extra effort in the sound.

## How to remember it

The **wick** is h with a twist — literally: the plain breath
wound tighter, ready to burn. Where *h* is an open door, *ḥ*
is a braided cord.

Piece by piece:

- **ḥwi̯** — beating: this wick opening the verb, the striking
  man doing the work
- **mḥꜥ** — the flax

A linen workshop on a tomb wall — and the wick-sign starts the
beating of the very fiber wicks are twisted from. The sign is
