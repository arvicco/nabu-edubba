## Where it comes from

A small round loaf of bread — the half-circle of dough every
Egyptian ate daily. It writes *t*, the single most frequent
sound-sign in the corpus: the feminine ending *-t* alone puts it
in half the nouns in the language.

## How to remember it

The **loaf** is daily bread — you will not read three words
without one. Every feminine word ends in this little bun; count
the loaves on any wall and you are counting the grammar.

Piece by piece:

- **ḏꜣ** — the fire-drill sign, lending its sound
- **-t** — this loaf, closing the bird's name

A crane from an offering list — poultry for the tomb table,
its name finished with bread. The sign is taught in
