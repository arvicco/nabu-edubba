## Where it comes from

A ring-stand for a jar — the household rack a round-bottomed
vessel sits in. It writes the sound *g*, hired out to any word
that needs it, from *gm* "find" to the *gꜣ* of "lack."

## How to remember it

A **stand** with no jar on it is the picture of wanting — and
Egyptian agrees: *gꜣ*, "lack," is spelled with this very sign.
The rack holds the sound *g* the way it holds a jar: snugly,
whatever gets set on it.

Piece by piece:

- **wni̯≡n** — we have grown indifferent
- **ꜥnḫ** — to life
- **m gꜣ≡k** — in the lack of you: the empty stand, the sad bird,
  the sickness-mark

Mourning, written with an empty jar stand at its heart. The sign
