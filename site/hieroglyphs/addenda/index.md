# Hieroglyphs Addenda

The numbered courses teach in sequence — each chapter assumes the
ones before it. The addenda work the other way: they are the
school's shelf of materials that serve *every* course and belong
to none, consulted whenever you need them, in any order, at any
point in your study.

## On the shelf

<ol class="course-toc">
  <li><a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/terms/' | relative_url }}">Egyptian terms</a>
      — the hieroglyph-specific glossary: uniliterals and
      cartouches, hieratic and serekh, one plain sentence each; the
      same definitions behind the hover bubbles</li>
  <li><a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/signs/' | relative_url }}">Signs — one page for every sign</a>
      — each taught sign's permanent home: its keyword, the picture
      Gardiner identified, a way to remember it, its lookalikes,
      and one real line it lives in</li>
  <li><a href="{{ '/hieroglyphs/addenda/drills/' | relative_url }}">Drills — every sign, three ways</a>
      — the whole inventory as retrieval cards, interleaved and
      printable as cut-out Leitner slips</li>
</ol>

Terms shared across all scripts — determinative, logogram, rebus,
and their kin — live in the site's
[general glossary]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}). The shelf will
grow — extended materials that outgrow a course chapter land here.
For the courses themselves, go back to the
[school catalog]({{ '/hieroglyphs/' | relative_url }}).
