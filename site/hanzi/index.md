# The Hanzi School

Hanzi (漢字) is the living logographic tradition on Edubba's roster — the
characters at the root of Chinese writing and, as Kanji, of Japanese
writing too. Unlike the ancient scripts on the roster, this tradition is
still in daily use by a huge number of readers today, which this school's
catalog will reflect: a foundations course on how the characters work,
then literacy tracks into Classical Chinese and into Japanese, with
further courses to follow. The goal, as with every Edubba school, is
genuine reading literacy — the ability to read real texts, not just facts
about the characters.

## Course catalog (planned)

- **101 · How hanzi work** — planned. The foundations course: how the
  characters work as a writing system. Prerequisite for everything below.
- **102 · Classical Chinese** — planned. A literacy track building toward
  reading real Classical Chinese texts.
- **103 · Japanese (kanji + kana)** — planned. A literacy track covering
  Japanese's use of kanji together with kana.
- **2xx onward** — planned. Further courses building on the foundations
  and literacy tracks above.

## A note on where things stand

No courses exist yet — this catalog is the plan, not a promise of what
has already been built. Numbering and scope will be firmed up as
curriculum design proceeds, following the cuneiform school's lead. If
you'd like to see the wider project, go back to
[the map of writing](/); if you have thoughts, corrections, or requests
for what should come first, they're welcome on
