# Like water

The chapters of this run taught rules, work, wanting, and the
state. The last word goes to likeness — being like, being the
same, following, and the good worth following:

若 began as someone smoothing their hair — yielding — and
settled into "to be like" (and, at a sentence's head, "if"). 同
holds many mouths under one cover. 從 is following, drawn twice
over. 善 has stood in a box twice in these chapters; it steps
out here. And 長 is read *cháng* for "long," *zhǎng* for "grow
up, elder" — one picture holding both ends of a life.

read every one, plus the line that explains them:

## The dark sameness

Whoever has blunted the edges and settled the dust, says the
*Laozi*, reaches a state it names with this chapter's 同:

<figure class="reading reading--script reading--stacked">
  <div class="reading-lines">
    <div class="reading-line"><span class="script">知者▢言，言者▢知。</span><span class="translit pinyin">zhī zhě fú yán, yán zhě fú zhī</span><span class="gloss">"those who know do not speak; those who speak do not know" — the famous warning label on all talk about the deepest things (posted, with a wink, at the head of a book of talk). Both boxes are the old "not"</span></div>

## The last rung of the ladder

The sixty-year ladder of Confucius' life has climbed through
these chapters since the counting days. 從 sets the final rung
in place:

<figure class="reading reading--script">
  <div class="reading-lines">

## The bow

One sentence to tie the whole run — the *Great Learning*
reaching from the ancients to the state, using this run's 欲,
先, and 國 in a single breath:

<figure class="reading reading--script">
  <div class="reading-lines">
    <div class="reading-line"><span class="script">古之欲明明▢於天下者，先▢其國。</span><span class="translit pinyin">gǔ zhī yù míng míng dé yú tiān xià zhě, xiān zhì qí guó</span><span class="gloss">"the ancients who wanted to brighten bright virtue in the whole world first set their own states in order" — start where your hands can reach. The boxes are "virtue" and "govern"</span></div>

## Where this leaves you

A hundred and thirty-seven characters — better than two of
every five on a classical page, and the count now includes the
hinge 則, the crew of doing, the economy of wanting, the
thousand-chariot state, and the water that beats them all by
not fighting. The course keeps going; the page keeps opening.
