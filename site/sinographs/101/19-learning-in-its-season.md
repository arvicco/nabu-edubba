word for learning. It arrives with the season to do it in:

學 is instruction drawn as a building: hands, rods, roof, child.
時 puts the sun on a schedule — 四時 are the four seasons, and
以時 "at the proper season" is how good government is measured.
年 carries the harvest home; 父 holds the household rod; 已
closes what is finished, and with 而 makes 而已 — "and that is
all." 安 seats a woman under a roof and calls it peace.

## The promise, paid

On the first page of this course you were promised the opening
of the *Analects*. You have been reading it in pieces since
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/10-the-sentences-tools/' | relative_url }}">chapter 10</a>;
its third clause arrived in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/17-lords-and-the-people/' | relative_url }}">chapter 17</a>.
Here it is whole — three clauses, three questions, six boxes in thirty

## Where this leaves you

A hundred and ten characters — one in every three characters of
the classical corpus, and the opening of the *Analects* read
whole. Ahead lie the words of consequence — *then* and *thus*,
getting and giving — and with them, longer and longer runs of
the texts these chapters have opened.
