# The thousand-chariot state

The people of the last chapters need somewhere to live — a
state, an age to live in, the ground under it, and the weather
of power that blows across it:

千 quietly repairs the course's number ladder — you have had
one through ten, a hundred, and even ten thousand since the
myriad chapter, but never the thousand between. 世 stacks three
tens into a generation. 地 completes the oldest pair in the
cosmos: 天地, heaven-and-earth, the classical name for
*everything there is*. And 風 blows two ways — real weather,
and the influence a ruler's example has on everyone downwind.

## Heaven and earth play no favorites

The pair 天地 arrives with the *Laozi*'s coldest, most bracing
line:

## The lord's virtue is wind

Asked whether he should execute the lawless for the sake of
order, a ruler gets this answer instead:

can be known, Confucius says yes — read the pattern:

## Where this leaves you

A hundred and thirty-two characters — the state governed, the
generations counted, the wide earth underfoot. One chapter
remains in this run: likeness — the highest good that is like
water, and a ladder sixty years long, climbed to its final
rung.
