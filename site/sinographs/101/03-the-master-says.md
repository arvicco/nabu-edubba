# The master says

Two last simple characters — the pair that opens half the canon —
and then the wall comes down: characters start combining.

Watch 曰 next to 日 the sun — the pair are the script's most
famous look-alikes. 曰 sits wider than tall, and its inner stroke
stops short of the right side; the sun is tall, and its inner
stroke runs wall to wall. The books will make you tell them apart
on every page, so start now.

## The master says

子 began as a drawing of a baby and never lost that sense — but in
the books you will read it is above all a **title**: *the Master*.
曰 is the tradition's opening quotation mark with a voice:
"said:". Put them together and you have
<span class="script">子曰</span>, *zǐ yuē*, "the Master said" — the
two-character hinge on which the entire *Analects* swings:

Every 子曰 you will ever meet — and the *Analects* alone has
hundreds — is now two characters of solid ground at the start of
the sentence. So is the first line of the *Spring and Autumn
Annals*, the chronicle that dates the world by the king you
learned on the first page:

## Built from what you own

Now look at the table's last four rows again. 明 is not a new
picture — it is 日 and 月 standing together. 旦 is 日 over 一. 休
is 人 against 木. 仁 is 人 beside 二. **Nothing there is new** —
only the combinations are. This is the third of the script's
moves: put two meanings together and let them make a third. How do
you draw *bright*? You don't; you set the sun and the moon side by
side. Seventeen simple characters were the price of admission, and
this is what they buy.

Notice 仁 and 人 say the same syllable, *rén* — the classics lean
on that pun on purpose ("humaneness is being-a-person"). Hold that
sound-echo: it is a clue to the fourth move, and the fourth move
is the whole rest of the script.

## Where this goes

Twenty-three characters. The last move remains — the one that
built nine of every ten characters in the dictionary: borrowing a
character for nothing but its **sound**. Next chapter, your tree
grows a root and a tip, and then it starts to sing.
