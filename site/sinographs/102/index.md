# Sinographs 102 · Literary Chinese

**The second course of the sinograph school.** It begins where
[101 · Foundations]({{ '/sinographs/101/' | relative_url }})
closed: you know how the characters work and own the 55 most
frequent of them; this course teaches the LANGUAGE they wrote —
Literary Chinese, the classical written tongue of the *Analects*,
the *Laozi*, the *Great Learning*, the *Odes*, and the *Changes*.

Each chapter teaches one element of the grammar — the sentence
shapes, the little words that steer them, the ways of negating,
asking, and concluding — and brings in the characters that
element needs, always on real lines of the real texts, cited to
their witnesses. By the end of the course the famous sayings you
met in pieces read whole, because you know both the characters
and the grammar holding them together.
