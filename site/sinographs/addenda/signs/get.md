## Where it comes from

The oldest forms show a hand closing on a cowrie shell — money
found — sometimes already with the road-step at the left:
picking up value on the way. The modern right half has worn the
shell past recognizing, but the left-hand road survives. Taught
in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/22-wanting-and-getting/' | relative_url }}">the wanting-and-getting chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A cowrie in the fist on the open road: **get**.
