## Where it comes from

A rising breath-mark, by the received account — the spoken
lift at the end of a classical question. Rank 136; with 不亦 it
makes the canon's gentlest rhetorical frame: "is it not…?"
Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/09-the-masters-opening/' | relative_url }}">the master's-opening chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The eyebrow at the end of the sentence: **really**? Hear the
pitch rise and let the question hang.
