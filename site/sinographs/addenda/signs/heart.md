## Where it comes from

The heart drawn with its chambers, four strokes. In classical
thought it is the organ of thinking as much as feeling — where
resolve, doubt, and understanding all live. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/07-doing-and-being/' | relative_url }}">the doing-and-being chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Three quick beats and a curve: the **heart**. When the
classics say "heart," read "mind" too — they never split the
two.
