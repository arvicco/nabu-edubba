## Where it comes from

One stroke for *one*: nothing is drawn, everything is pointed
at. It is also the single most-written stroke of the script — the
horizontal that opens 大, 天, 王 and hundreds more. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>
at the head of the number batch.

## How to remember it

Lay one finger flat on the table. That is the whole character —
**one**, and the first stroke of half the script.
