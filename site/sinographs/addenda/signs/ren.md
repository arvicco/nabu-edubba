## Where it comes from

A person walking, seen from the side — two legs and a leaning
back. The oracle-bone forms already show exactly this figure, and
it has never been anything else: 人 is one of the script's few
characters whose picture-story is simply true. It is also the
seed of a family: spread the arms and it is 大; that story is
told in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">the chapter that teaches it</a>.

## How to remember it

Nothing to invent — two strokes, two legs, a **person**
mid-stride. If you ever forget, stand up and take a step.
