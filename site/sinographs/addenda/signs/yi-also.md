## Where it comes from

A person — your 大 shape — with two small marks under the
arms: "armpits," by the received account, borrowed whole for
"also, likewise." Rank 61, and one half of the canon's warmest
formula, 不亦…乎. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/09-the-masters-opening/' | relative_url }}">the master's-opening chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Everyone has two of them, one on each side: **also**. The
armpit character says "likewise" — anatomy retired into
grammar.
