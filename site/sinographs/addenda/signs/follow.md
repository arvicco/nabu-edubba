## Where it comes from

The heart of the graph is just two person-signs, one walking
behind the other — following, drawn twice over. The road-step
on the left and the foot below joined later to make the walking
explicit. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

One walker behind another: **follow**.
