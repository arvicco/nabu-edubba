## Where it comes from

An eye 目 set on running legs — a whole person reduced to the
act of looking; the reading is classic. When what is seen does
the appearing, the classics read the same graph xiàn, "to show
oneself". Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

The eye grew legs and went to **see** for itself.
