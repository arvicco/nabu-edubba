## Where it comes from

Three strokes, the middle shortest — and the end of the pattern:
four is a borrowed character, not four strokes, because the script
felt the cliff coming and switched moves. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Count the strokes and you have read the character: **three**.
Enjoy it — it is the last time the trick works.
