## Where it comes from

A person 亻 stands beside the officer-with-tally sign — someone
is being given the job. To send on a mission, to employ, and in
grammar the causer: 使 X do Y, "make X do Y." An envoy is this
character with a title. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/12-making-it-happen/' | relative_url }}">the making-it-happen chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A thin person at the left, the official with his tally at the
right: hand someone the job and **send** them — or, in grammar,
*make them do it*.
