## Where it comes from

A winnowing basket on its stand — the classic borrowed
picture: the basket later took itself a new character, and the
pronoun kept this one. Rank 6 of the corpus: his, her, its,
their. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/09-the-masters-opening/' | relative_url }}">the master's-opening chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A basket that holds everything and owns nothing of it:
**its**. The commonest possessive in the classics rides a piece
of farm equipment.
