## Where it comes from

A dancer with sleeves full of tassels, borrowed for the word
"there is not" — the dance later took itself a new character and
left this one holding pure absence. Rank 21, and classical
philosophy's favorite word. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/07-doing-and-being/' | relative_url }}">the doing-and-being chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

See today's strokes as a woodpile over a row of fire-dots —
everything burned away. Twelve strokes of costume and no dancer
left inside: **without**. The fullest-looking character in the
chapter means nothing at all — and that is how you remember it.
