## Where it comes from

A person beside two: what holds *between* two people — the
received account of the deepest virtue in the Confucian world.
It says the same syllable as 人, and the classics lean on the pun
on purpose: humaneness is being-a-person. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/03-the-master-says/' | relative_url }}">the master's chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

One person, then the number two: it takes two people for
**humane** to mean anything. The character is an ethics lecture
in five strokes.
