## Where it comes from

A cowrie shell drawn face-on, its two feelers trailing below —
the oldest forms are unmistakably the shell. Cowries were money
before coins were cast, and the graph kept the job after the
shells retired: characters about value, goods, and trade wear 貝
as their marker. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

A shell with feelers: **cowrie** — the money that came before
money.
