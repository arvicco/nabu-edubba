## Where it comes from

A blade with its curved handle, cut to two strokes — one of the
script's plainest pictures. On the right side of a compound it
slims to 刂, the same knife drawn thin; cutting, dividing, and
judging words carry it. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/10-if-and-then/' | relative_url }}">the if-and-then chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Two strokes, one edge: **knife**.
