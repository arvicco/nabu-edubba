## Where it comes from

A stream in mid-current: the long center line is the flow, the
strokes beside it the eddies. Squeezed to the side of a character
it becomes the three quick drops 氵 — the shape-change taught with
沐. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

**Water** running fast enough to braid — the middle stroke is
the current, the side strokes the spray.
