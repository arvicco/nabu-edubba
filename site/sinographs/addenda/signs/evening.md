## Where it comes from

The moon 月 with one stroke withheld — the sliver that rises
while the sky is still light: evening. Pinned as the component 名
needs. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/08-stopping-at-true/' | relative_url }}">the stopping-at-true chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Not yet the full moon of night — just its first sliver:
**evening**. One stroke short of 月, one hour short of dark.
