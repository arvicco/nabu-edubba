## Where it comes from

古 "old" lends both its sound and its sense; the tapping hand 攵
turns it into a happening — the classic build. What stands
behind a thing is its precedent; the precedent is its cause; and
"from this cause" hardened into the classical hinge-word
*therefore*, the word every argument in the classics turns on.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/15-and-so/' | relative_url }}">the and-so chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The old 古, hand at work: what was done before is the reason
now — **therefore**.
