## Where it comes from

八 began life meaning "apart" — two strokes leaning away from
each other — before the number eight borrowed it. Set the knife
刀 beneath and the splitting has its tool: 分, to divide; also
the share that division hands you. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/10-if-and-then/' | relative_url }}">the if-and-then chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Apart, by knife: **divide**.
