## Where it comes from

An open mouth, squared off by the brush the way the sun-disc
was. As a component it is the single busiest meaning-part in the
script — anything said, eaten, tasted, or shouted tends to carry
it; this course alone builds 品 and 味 on it. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

An open **mouth**, mid-word. Guard it well — the classics
never stop saying so.
