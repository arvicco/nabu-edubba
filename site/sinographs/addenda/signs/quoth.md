## Where it comes from

The original cloud pictograph — curling vapor under a level
sky; the reading is classic. Borrowed early for the quotative
verb, it kept the job so firmly that clouds had to take a rain
roof (雲) to stay distinct. 曰 speaks in a person's own voice;
云 hands over someone else's — the Songs say, the Book says.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/13-liking-and-likening/' | relative_url }}">the liking-and-likening chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Words drift in from elsewhere like a cloud: **quoth**.
