## Where it comes from

A stroke planted through the very center of a box — the old
forms show a pole or banner set mid-camp, the point everything
musters on. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>
with the other pointing marks.

## How to remember it

Plant the flag dead center. **Middle** — and later, *within*:
what is at the middle of a thing is inside it.
