## Where it comes from

The foot 止 stopped at the line 一: arriving exactly where one
should. "Upright, correct" — and, adverbially, "exactly." Rank 87,
and the moral vocabulary's favorite character. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/08-stopping-at-true/' | relative_url }}">the stopping-at-true chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Toe the line and you are **upright** — the character is a
posture and a principle in five strokes.
