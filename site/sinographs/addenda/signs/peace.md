## Where it comes from

A woman 女 seated under the roof — the classic account: the
household settled, the house at rest. The classics also borrow
it as a question word ("wherein? how?"), but its heart is
peace, ease, safety — the thing good government owes the old.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

Roof over, seated within: **peace**.
