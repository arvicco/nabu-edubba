## Where it comes from

A line drawn over the big person's head — what is above even the
greatest. That is the traditional reading, going back to the
*Shuowen*; the oracle-bone forms show the figure with a heavy,
squared head, which modern paleography takes as the original
picture (the head, then the sky over it — the accounts differ,
and the character is older than both). Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">the opening chapter</a>
as the last link of 人 → 大 → 天.

## How to remember it

The big person reaches up; the one stroke they cannot reach past
is **heaven**. Everything in the classical books happens under
that line.
