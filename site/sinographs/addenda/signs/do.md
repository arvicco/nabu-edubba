## Where it comes from

The old form draws a hand leading an elephant — work itself,
in one picture — and the language borrowed it for its plainest
verb: do, make, act as. Rank eight of the corpus. Read *wéi* as
the verb; read *wèi* when it means "for the sake of." Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/07-doing-and-being/' | relative_url }}">the doing-and-being chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A claw at the top, four small feet at the bottom: what is left
of the hand leading the elephant — the heaviest **doing** the
script ever drew.
