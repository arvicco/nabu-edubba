## Where it comes from

A mound of earth standing on the ground-line — by the received
account, an earthen altar: the heaped soil at which a community
worshipped the land itself. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">the opening chapter</a>
as the floor of the course's cosmology: heaven above, earth below.

## How to remember it

A shovelful of **earth** patted into a mound on flat ground.
Everything in the classical world stands on this stroke.
