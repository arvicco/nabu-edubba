## Where it comes from

The oracle bones drew a level eye with its iris; when the script
straightened into columns the eye stood up on end — the classic
account. It anchors the whole seeing family: on legs it is 見,
before a tree it is 相. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

An **eye** stood upright: lids top and bottom, iris barred in
the middle.
