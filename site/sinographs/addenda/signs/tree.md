## Where it comes from

A trunk with branches reaching up and roots reaching down. As a
component it is the busiest of the course's pictures: mark its
base and you have 本 the root, its top and you have 末 the tip,
let it lend its sound and you have 沐. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A child's drawing of a **tree** — arms up for branches, legs
down for roots. Half of
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/04-roots-tips-borrowed-sounds/' | relative_url }}">chapter 04</a>
grows on it.
