## Where it comes from

The sun 日 over the upright 正: what stands squarely so, by
the received account. Rank 30; in the classics it points — "this,
the very one" — and judges: "right, so." Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/08-stopping-at-true/' | relative_url }}">the stopping-at-true chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Sunlight falling straight on the mark: **this** one, exactly
so. When later Chinese needed a plain word for "is," it reached
for this character.
