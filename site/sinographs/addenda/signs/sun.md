## Where it comes from

The sun-disc, squared off by the brush, the old dot of light
kept as the middle stroke. As a word it is both the sun and the
day the sun makes. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>,
and immediately put to work as a component: 明, 旦, and dozens more
are built on it.

## How to remember it

A window with the **sun** caught in it — tall, bright, its
inner stroke running wall to wall (that is how you tell it from
曰, the mouth that speaks).
