## Where it comes from

Evening 夕 beside mouth 口: when the light is gone and your
face is not enough, you say your name — the received story, and a
good one. Rank 69: "name," and the verb "to name." Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/08-stopping-at-true/' | relative_url }}">the stopping-at-true chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A voice in the dusk: "It is I." **Name** — the word you keep
for when you cannot be seen.
