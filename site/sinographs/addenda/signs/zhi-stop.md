## Where it comes from

A footprint — toes and heel — the foot at rest. "To stop," and
in the classics also "where one comes to rest": one's proper
place. Seeds 正 and, through it, 是. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/08-stopping-at-true/' | relative_url }}">the stopping-at-true chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

One footprint, going nowhere: **stop**. The classics make an
ethics of it — knowing where to stop is half of wisdom.
