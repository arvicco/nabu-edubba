## Where it comes from

A right hand seen from the side, two strokes — one of the
script's oldest and busiest components. As a word it means
"again, further"; as a part it grips inside 有 and dozens of
hand-characters to come. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/05-the-busiest-words/' | relative_url }}">the busiest-words chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The hand that reaches back for more: **again**. Whenever a
character needs a hand, look for these two strokes.
