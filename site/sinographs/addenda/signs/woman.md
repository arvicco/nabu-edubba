## Where it comes from

A figure kneeling with hands folded before her — the
oracle-bone posture, kept recognizable through three thousand
years of rewriting; the reading is classic. She anchors a whole
family: beside a child she is 好, beside a mouth she lends her
old sound to 如. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

The kneeling figure, hands crossed: **woman** — the script's
most steadfast pictograph.
