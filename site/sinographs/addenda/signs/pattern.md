## Where it comes from

A standing figure, chest marked — the stated account: the oldest
graphs draw crossed lines, sometimes a heart, on the body: a
tattooed pattern. From "pattern" the word spread to every
patterned thing — the grain of wood, the tracks of birds, the
stars (天文, the sky's pattern) — and above all to writing,
until 文 meant the whole civil, lettered order of the world.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

A figure standing tall with a **pattern** on its chest — culture
worn on the body.
