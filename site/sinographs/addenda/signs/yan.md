## Where it comes from

The mouth 口 under rising lines — sound on its way out, by
the received account. Rank 40 as the word "speech, to speak,"
and the meaning-component of the script's whole talking family.
Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/06-knowing-and-walking/' | relative_url }}">the knowing-and-walking chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The mouth, mid-sentence — lines of **speech** already leaving
it. Wherever you see it packed into a character, someone is
talking.
