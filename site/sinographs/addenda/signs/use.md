## Where it comes from

The old picture is honestly disputed — scholars have seen a
bucket, a fence of planks, a hung bell struck in ceremony. The
word it writes was never in doubt: *to use, to put to work*.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/10-the-sentences-tools/' | relative_url }}">the sentence's-tools chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A frame of slats, ready to hand — whatever it once pictured,
you can **use** it.
