## Where it comes from

A sprout rising from the ground-line — growth drawn in five
strokes. Rank 53: to grow, to be born, to give rise to; as a
noun, life itself. The verb of the Laozi's whole cosmology.
Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/07-doing-and-being/' | relative_url }}">the doing-and-being chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

One shoot above the soil: **grow**. Everything the Way gives
birth to, it gives birth to through this character.
