## Where it comes from

The tally that closed a count of ten — early forms are a
single weighted vertical, later crossed into the familiar shape.
Classical numbers compose on it: 二十 twenty, 三十 thirty.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/05-the-busiest-words/' | relative_url }}">the busiest-words chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

One stroke down, one across: the count is full. **Ten** — and
every multiple of ten stacks a digit in front of it.
