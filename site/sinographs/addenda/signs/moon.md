## Where it comes from

A crescent with its inner shading — the moon is always drawn
waxing, never full, so it can never be mistaken for the sun. As a
word it is the moon and the month the moon measures. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The **moon** with two shadows resting inside its crescent.
Whenever a character needs night, light, or a month, look for this
shape at its side.
