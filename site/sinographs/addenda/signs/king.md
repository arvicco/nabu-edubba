## Where it comes from

The reading the tradition loved: three level strokes — heaven
above, earth below, humankind between — joined by the one vertical
that runs through all three; the king is what connects the levels.
The earliest forms look more like an axe blade, the plain emblem of
power, and the course says so honestly. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">the opening chapter</a>
as the roof-beam of the cosmology.

## How to remember it

Three floors, one pillar. Whoever holds the pillar is the
**king** — the character is a political theory in four strokes.
