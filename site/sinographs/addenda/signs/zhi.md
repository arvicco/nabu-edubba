## Where it comes from

The most-written character in classical Chinese — rank one of
the whole corpus. The early form draws a foot setting out over a
line; the grammar word *zhī* ("of"; "him, her, it") borrowed the
picture for its sound and kept it forever. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/05-the-busiest-words/' | relative_url }}">the busiest-words chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Three quick strokes, everywhere. X 之 Y is "the Y **of** X" —
read 之 as the hinge of the phrase and half of classical syntax
opens.
