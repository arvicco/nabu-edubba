## Where it comes from

What the graph once drew is disputed, and this course says so.
Its classical work is beyond dispute: rank 10, the particle that
turns any verb into its doer — 知者 the one who knows, 仁者 the
humane one. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/09-the-masters-opening/' | relative_url }}">the master's-opening chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Hang 者 after a verb and a person appears: the one **who**
does it. The character is a job title generator.
