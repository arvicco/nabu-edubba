## Where it comes from

Three peaks in a row, the middle one tallest — a pictograph
that has barely changed in three thousand years. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/02-the-world-in-pictures/' | relative_url }}">the pictures chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A **mountain** range you could draw with three strokes of a
stick — because that is exactly what it is.
