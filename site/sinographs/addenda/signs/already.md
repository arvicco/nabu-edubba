## Where it comes from

Graphically a twin of 巳, whose own picture is debated — a
serpent, an unborn child — and the borrowing is old beyond
tracing: 已 means what is finished, done, already so. With 而
it makes the classics' favorite full stop: 而已, "and that is
all." Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

The curl closes on itself: **already** done.
