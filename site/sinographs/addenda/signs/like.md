## Where it comes from

女 beside a mouth 口 — the stated account hears 女's Old
Chinese sound carrying the word. The uses fan out from
likeness: X 如 Y "X is like Y"; 不如 "falls short of"; 如…何
"what is to be done with…"; and at a clause's head, "if".
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/13-like-and-alike/' | relative_url }}">the like-and-alike chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Say it **like** she said it — the mouth repeating what it
heard.
