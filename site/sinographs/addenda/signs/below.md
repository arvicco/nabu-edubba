## Where it comes from

The 上 gesture flipped: the mark hangs under the line.
*Belowness*, pointed at — and in the word 天下, "all under heaven,"
it names the whole world. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The ground line, and something **below** it — the root cellar
of the pointing pair.
