## Where it comes from

The hand 又 holding meat — the meat-shape came to be written
like the moon 月, and the merged form stuck. Rank nine of the
corpus: "to have," and the classical "there is." Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/05-the-busiest-words/' | relative_url }}">the busiest-words chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

What the hand holds, you **have**. Hand above, meat below —
possession drawn in six strokes.
