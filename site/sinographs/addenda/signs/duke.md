## Where it comes from

Dividing 八 above a closed circle meaning "private" — Han Fei's
stated reading: to turn one's back on the private is the
public. The word carries both senses at once in the classics:
the feudal rank ("Duke of Zhou") and everything held in common.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/12-making-it-happen/' | relative_url }}">the making-it-happen chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The parting strokes push the private circle away: what is not
kept for oneself is **public** — and the duke is supposed to
embody it.
