## Where it comes from

What 也 once pictured is honestly disputed — the scholars
disagree, and this course says so. Its classical life is
unambiguous: the final particle that closes a statement, the
struck bell at the end of "X is Y 也." Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/05-the-busiest-words/' | relative_url }}">the busiest-words chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The full stop that speaks: **indeed**, it is so. When you
hear the bell, the sentence is over.
