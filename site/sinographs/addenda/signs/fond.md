## Where it comes from

Woman 女 and child 子 side by side — the classic account of
what *good* looks like. Read hǎo it is the adjective; read hào,
the classical texts' favorite, it is the verb: *to love, to be
fond of*. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/13-like-and-alike/' | relative_url }}">the like-and-alike chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Mother and child together: **fond** — the picture explains both
the noun's warmth and the verb's pull.
