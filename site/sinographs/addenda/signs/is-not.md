## Where it comes from

Two wings spread back to back — turned away from each other;
the stated account reads contrariety straight off the picture.
Where 不 negates a verb ("does not"), 非 negates a whole claim:
非 X, "it is not X, it is no X of yours" — and from "contrary
to right" comes the sense *wrong*. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

Wings flying apart: whatever you claimed, this **is not** it.
