## Where it comes from

The mouth 口 for the saying, the arrow 矢 close beside the
sound: the received account hears knowing as the word that flies
straight to its mark. Rank 52, and the classical verb of
understanding. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/06-knowing-and-walking/' | relative_url }}">the knowing-and-walking chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

What you **know**, you can say as straight as an arrow flies.
Arrow plus mouth — aim before you speak.
