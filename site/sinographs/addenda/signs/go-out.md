## Where it comes from

The old graphs draw a footprint climbing over the lip of a pit
or dwelling-hollow — the stated account: stepping OUT. Worn
smooth, the foot now looks like twin sprouts, but the word
never wavered: emergence, exit, and everything that comes
forth — words from mouths, troops from gates, the sun from the
east. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

A foot over the pit's edge: **going out**.
