## Where it comes from

An arrow, drawn whole: head at the top, shaft, fletching at the
base. Rare on its own in the classics, but pinned early because
知 is built on it — components before compounds. Taught in <a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/06-knowing-and-walking/' | relative_url }}">the knowing-and-walking chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Five strokes, one **arrow** — nocked and ready. Its real work
is inside other characters: listen for shǐ, look for the point.
