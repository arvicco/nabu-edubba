## Where it comes from

Your sun 日 just clear of the horizon line 一: daybreak, drawn.
The classics date mornings by it — 旦日, "the next morning."
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/03-the-master-says/' | relative_url }}">the master's chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The sun sitting on the horizon — **dawn**. One line lower and
it would still be night.
