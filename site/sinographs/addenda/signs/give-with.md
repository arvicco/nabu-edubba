## Where it comes from

Four hands — two above, two below — pass a single object between
them: giving and taking part in one picture. Grammar wore it
down to the word between nouns: X 與 Y, "X and Y, X with Y."
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/07-with-by-from-at/' | relative_url }}">the coverb chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Hands at the four corners, one object held in the middle:
giving, taking, sharing in one frame — **give-with**.
