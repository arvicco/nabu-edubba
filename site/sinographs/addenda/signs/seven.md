## Where it comes from

A horizontal stroke cut through by another — the stated account
makes it the original of "to cut," which later grew a knife of
its own to keep that job. The graph itself was lent to the
number seven so early that no classical text remembers it
meaning anything else. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

One stroke **cut** by another: seven, the number that won't
divide.
