## Where it comes from

A nose drawn face-on — bridge, nostrils and all; the reading is
classic. Pointing at one's own nose to mean "me" is a gesture
Chinese speakers still make, and the graph froze it. The sense
"from" (a starting point — where the self stands) rides along.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

The nose you point at when you say *me*: **self** — and every
journey measured *from* it.
