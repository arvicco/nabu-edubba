## Where it comes from

The third most frequent character in the classical corpus. The
old graph's picture is honestly disputed — one reading sees a
person carrying something, another an implement — and the word
grammaticalized so early that the classics use it almost purely
as a tool-word: 以 X, "by X, using X". Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/07-with-by-from-at/' | relative_url }}">the coverb chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

A person bends to pick something up: whatever it is, the
sentence will now be done **by means of** it.
