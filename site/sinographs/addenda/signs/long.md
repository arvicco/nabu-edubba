## Where it comes from

An elder with long, unbound hair leaning on a staff — the hair
is the length. Read *cháng*, it is "long"; read *zhǎng*, it is
"to grow, the elder, the chief" — one picture holding both ends
of a life. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/13-like-and-alike/' | relative_url }}">the like-and-alike chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Hair streaming out stroke by stroke past its pin — a lifetime's
growth: **long**. Read the same picture as the elder it grew on
and you have *zhǎng*, "senior."
