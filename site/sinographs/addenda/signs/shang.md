## Where it comes from

A short mark set above the baseline: *aboveness*, pointed at.
With 下 it forms the script's neatest pair — one gesture, flipped.
Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

The ground line, and something **above** it. Flip the page
upside down and you have written its opposite.
