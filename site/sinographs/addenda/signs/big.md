## Where it comes from

A person — the same figure as 人 — with the arms thrown out wide:
*this* big. The reading is uncontroversial and old; the character
is the middle link of the chain taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/00-orientation/' | relative_url }}">the opening chapter</a>,
between the walking person and the heaven over both.

## How to remember it

Ask a child how big the fish was. The arms go out; that gesture,
frozen into three strokes, is **big**.
