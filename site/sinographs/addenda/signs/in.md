## Where it comes from

An old form of the crow 烏, borrowed whole for the preposition
— the stated account. It is the SAME word as
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/addenda/signs/at/' | relative_url }}">于</a>
in a fuller dress: the oldest songs and annals point with 于,
the *Analects* and the philosophers prefer 於. In, at, on —
and after a comparison, *than*. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

The crow lands wherever it likes: **in**, at, on.
