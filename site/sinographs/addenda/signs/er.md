## Where it comes from

Two strokes for *two*, the upper drawn a little shorter — the
pointing move at its plainest. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/01-the-order-of-strokes/' | relative_url }}">the strokes chapter</a>,
one breath after 一.

## How to remember it

Two fingers now. The upper stroke is shorter because the brush
is already moving on — **two**, and gone.
