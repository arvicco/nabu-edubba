## Where it comes from

An eye 目 examining a tree 木 — the stated account hears the
forester sizing up timber. The appraising look became the
classical adverb of reciprocity: 相 before a verb makes it run
both ways. Read xiàng, the same graph names the minister who
inspects for the throne. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/' | relative_url }}">102 · Literary Chinese</a>.

## How to remember it

Eye meets tree, look meets look: **mutual**.
