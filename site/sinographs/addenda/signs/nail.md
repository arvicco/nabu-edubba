## Where it comes from

Two strokes: a nail seen head-on and driven flush. The stated
reading goes back to the nail-head picture; the word also names
an able-bodied man (a "stout nail" of a household) and the
fourth of the ten day-stems the calendar counts by. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/101/10-the-sentences-tools/' | relative_url }}">the sentence's-tools chapter</a>
as the right-hand piece of 可.

## How to remember it

The simplest hardware in the writing system: a **nail**. Hold
the head, drive the point.
