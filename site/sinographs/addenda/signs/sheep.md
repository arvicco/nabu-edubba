## Where it comes from

A ram seen head-on — the classic identification: the top pair of
strokes are the horns curling from the crown, and the vertical
runs down through the bars of the face to the muzzle. The sheep
stood beside the ox among the great sacrificial animals, and as
a component it lends its head to characters of goodness and
plenty — the flock was wealth you could eat. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/03-having-and-lacking/' | relative_url }}">the having-and-lacking chapter</a>,
beside its barnyard twin 牛.

## How to remember it

牛's partner, horns turned the other way: the ox's horns sweep
up, the ram's curl down. Same face-on head, one bar more.
