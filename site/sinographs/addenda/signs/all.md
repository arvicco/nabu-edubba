## Where it comes from

Two people stand side by side (the pair 比) over a saying-mark,
which the received graph writes with the white-sign 白: everyone
speaking with one voice — all, without exception. Taught in
<a href="{{ '/sinographs/102/14-all-of-it/' | relative_url }}">the all-of-it chapter</a>.

## How to remember it

Two figures shoulder to shoulder on top, 白 beneath: everyone
lined up saying one plain thing — **all**.
